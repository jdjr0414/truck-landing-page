Common Questions

What commercial trucks can I finance?

Semi trucks, dump trucks, box trucks, flatbeds, tankers, refrigerated trucks, tow trucks, cement trucks, logging trucks, and service trucks. Each has a dedicated financing page.

How much do commercial trucks cost?

Semi: $120K–$200K new. Dump: $80K–$180K. Box: $35K–$80K. Flatbed: $80K–$150K. Tanker: similar to semi. See equipment cost pages for details.

What industries use commercial trucks?

Freight, logistics, construction, hauling, towing, delivery, oilfield, materials. See industry pages for sector-specific financing.
