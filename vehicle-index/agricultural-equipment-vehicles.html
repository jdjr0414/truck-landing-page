Agricultural Equipment Vehicles

Commercial vehicles used in farming and agribusiness: grain haulers, dump trucks, flatbeds, tankers, and farm service trucks. Each page covers financing requirements, typical costs, and terms.

Agriculture Vehicle Types and Use Cases

Semi trucks are used for grain hauling from farm to elevator or processor. Cost: $120k�$200k new. See Semi Truck Financing.

Dump trucks haul aggregate, feed, and materials on and off the farm. Cost: $80k�$180k new. See Dump Truck Financing.

Flatbeds and tankers move equipment, supplies, and liquids. See Flatbed Truck Financing and Tanker Truck Financing.

Agricultural tractors, sprayers, and crop dusters are specialized farm equipment. Tractors: $80k�$200k+. Sprayers: $150k�$400k+. Crop dusters (aircraft): $500k�$2M+. See Agricultural Tractor Financing, Agricultural Sprayer Financing, and Crop Duster Financing. Lenders may account for seasonal revenue when structuring terms.

Choosing the Right Agriculture Vehicle

Grain haulers need semi trucks. Dump trucks haul aggregate and materials. Flatbeds move equipment and supplies. Tankers haul liquids. Service trucks support field operations. Tractors and sprayers are specialized farm equipment. Crop dusters (aircraft) require separate aviation financing. Match the vehicle to your operation and discuss seasonal cash flow with your lender. See Agriculture Equipment Financing.

Approval Timeline

Agriculture equipment financing typically takes 24�72 hours. Seasonal revenue may affect structure; discuss cash flow with your lender. Tractors and sprayers have strong collateral value.

Common Questions

What trucks do farmers and agribusiness use?

Semi trucks for grain hauling, dump trucks for aggregate, flatbeds for equipment and supplies, tankers for liquids, and service trucks for field support.

Can farmers finance commercial trucks?

Yes. Agriculture equipment financing is available. Lenders may account for seasonal revenue. Typical terms 36�72 months. See agriculture equipment financing industry page.

How much do farm trucks cost?

Semi: $120K�$200K. Dump: $80K�$180K. Flatbed: $80K�$150K. Tanker: similar to semi. Service truck: $50K�$120K. Costs vary by type and configuration.

Do lenders offer seasonal payment plans for farm equipment?

Some lenders structure payments around harvest or offer seasonal plans. Discuss cash flow with your lender when applying.

Can I finance a used agricultural tractor?

Yes. Used tractor financing is available. Terms typically 36�60 months depending on age and hours.
