Common Questions

What trucks do farmers and agribusiness use?

Semi trucks for grain hauling, dump trucks for aggregate, flatbeds for equipment and supplies, tankers for liquids, and service trucks for field support.

Can farmers finance commercial trucks?

Yes. Agriculture equipment financing is available. Lenders may account for seasonal revenue. Typical terms 36–72 months. See agriculture equipment financing industry page.

How much do farm trucks cost?

Varies by type. Semi: $120K–$200K. Dump: $80K–$180K. Flatbed: $80K–$150K. Tanker: similar to semi. Service truck: $50K–$120K.
