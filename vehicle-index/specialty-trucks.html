Common Questions

What are specialty trucks?

Trucks with vocational equipment: vac trucks, hydro excavation, crane trucks, boom trucks, airport fire, rail maintenance. Used for environmental, utility, and industrial work.

How much do specialty trucks cost?

Vac trucks: $80K–$350K. Hydro excavation: $150K–$350K. Crane trucks: $100K–$350K. Boom trucks: $90K–$250K. See equipment cost pages.

Can I finance a vac truck or hydro excavation truck?

Yes. Vac truck and hydro excavation financing is widely available. Typical terms 48–72 months. Down payment 10–30%.
