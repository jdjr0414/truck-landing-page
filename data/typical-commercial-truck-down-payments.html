Typical Commercial Truck Down Payments

Typical commercial truck down payments range from 0–35% depending on credit, equipment type, and new vs. used. Strong credit often qualifies with little or no down payment; startups and lower credit may need 20–35%. This page summarizes typical percentages and example dollar amounts by truck type.

Key Takeaways

  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment
  • Financing terms commonly range from 36-60 months

How Down Payment Affects Your Loan

Larger down payments reduce loan amount, monthly payment, and total interest. On a $150,000 truck: 10% down = $135,000 loan; 25% down = $112,500 loan. At 10% APR over 60 months, the difference is ~$500/month and ~$13,500 in total interest. Down payment also affects approval–stronger applications may qualify with less. See Down Payment Requirements.

Trade-In and Down Payment

Trade-in value counts toward down payment. If your current truck is worth $40,000 and you owe $20,000, you have $20,000 in equity toward the next purchase. Negative equity (truck worth less than balance) is difficult to roll into a new loan for commercial trucks. Pay down the existing loan or sell privately to clear the balance before upgrading.

Common Questions

What is the typical down payment for a commercial truck?

15–25% when required. Strong credit may qualify with 0–10%. Lower credit: 20–35%.

Can I get truck financing with no down payment?

Yes. Strong credit and established businesses often qualify with $0 down. Requirements are risk-based.

Do used trucks require larger down payments?

Often 15–25% vs. 10–20% for new. Older or higher-mileage units may require more.

Does a larger down payment lower my rate?

Often yes. Larger down payments can qualify for better APRs and lower monthly payments.

What credit score gets the lowest down payment?

720+ often qualifies with 0–10%. 680–719: 10–20%. Below 650: 20–35%.
