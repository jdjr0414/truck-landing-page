Typical Truck Financing Rates

Truck financing rates vary by credit, equipment type, and term. Prime borrowers often see 6–10% APR; average credit 10–16%; lower credit 14–22%. This page summarizes typical APR ranges and factors that affect your rate.

Key Takeaways

  • Financing terms commonly range from 60-84 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

Factors That Affect Your Rate

  • Credit score
  • Down payment size
  • Equipment type (new vs. used)
  • Term length
  • Business revenue history

Equipment type: Semi trucks and dump trucks have strong resale markets; lenders often offer better rates. Vocational equipment (bucket trucks, vac trucks) may have slightly higher rates due to narrower collateral markets. New equipment typically qualifies for 1–3% lower APR than used.

Term length: Longer terms (72–84 months) may carry slightly higher rates than 48–60 months. Shorter terms reduce total interest but increase monthly payment.

Payment Impact Example

On a $100,000 loan (after 20% down on $125,000 truck): 8% APR over 60 months ≈ $2,027/month, total interest ≈ $21,600. At 14% APR: ≈ $2,327/month, total interest ≈ $39,600. A 6% rate difference adds ~$300/month and ~$18,000 in interest over the term. See How to Finance Commercial Trucks for steps to improve your rate.

New vs Used Rate Spread

Used truck loans often run 1–3% higher than new. Lenders price for higher collateral risk (depreciation, condition uncertainty). Strong credit can narrow the gap. Used equipment also typically has shorter terms (24–60 months), which affects total cost.

Common Questions

What credit score gets the best truck financing rates?

720+ typically qualifies for best rates (6–10%). 680–719: competitive. Below 680: higher APRs.

Does down payment affect truck financing rates?

Yes. Larger down payments often qualify for better rates. Strong credit and down payment together improve terms.

Do startups get higher truck financing rates?

Often yes. Startups lack revenue history. Lenders may charge 1–3% more. Strong personal credit can offset.

How long are truck loan terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 24–60 months. Longer terms may have slightly higher rates.

How fast can I get truck financing?

Approval speed is independent of rate. Simple applications: 1–3 business days.

Do used truck loans have higher rates?

Often 1–3% higher than new. Shorter terms also common for used equipment.

What documentation affects my rate?

Tax returns, bank statements, and P&L show revenue. Strong revenue supports better rates. Lenders evaluate full profile.

What is the typical APR for truck financing?

6–12% for qualified borrowers. Prime borrowers: 6–10%. Subprime: 12–25%.

Are truck loan rates higher than auto loans?

Often yes. Commercial truck loans typically have higher rates than consumer auto loans.

What affects my truck financing rate?

Credit score, down payment, equipment type, term length, and business history.
