Commercial Truck Depreciation Chart

Commercial trucks depreciate over time. Semi tractors typically lose 15–25% in year one, then 10–15% annually. Vocational and specialty trucks vary by type and use. This page summarizes typical depreciation patterns and factors that affect resale value.

Factors That Affect Depreciation

  • Mileage or engine hours
  • Condition and maintenance
  • Make and model reputation
  • Emissions tier (older may depreciate faster)
  • Market demand for used equipment

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a semi truck depreciate per year?

Semi tractors typically lose 15–25% in year one, then 10–15% annually.

Do vocational trucks depreciate faster than semis?

Depends on type. Heavy-use vocational may depreciate faster. Well-maintained specialty units can hold value.

What affects commercial truck depreciation?

Mileage, hours, condition, make/model, emissions tier, and market demand.

When does a truck depreciate the most?

First 1–2 years typically see the steepest depreciation.
