Average Cost of Commercial Trucks

Commercial truck costs vary by type, age, and specs. This page summarizes average price ranges for semi tractors, dump trucks, bucket trucks, vocational vehicles, and specialty equipment. Use these ranges for budgeting and financing planning.

Key Takeaways

  • Semi: $120k–$200k new; dump: $80k–$150k; bucket: $80k–$250k; vac: $80k–$200k
  • Used prices typically 40–60% of new; age and mileage affect valuation
  • See individual cost pages for configuration-specific pricing

Quick Answer

Average commercial truck costs vary by type: semi tractors $120k–$200k new ($50k–$120k used), dump trucks $80k–$150k new ($45k–$90k used), bucket trucks $80k–$250k new ($40k–$120k used), vac trucks $80k–$200k new ($40k–$120k used). Fire trucks and garbage trucks run higher. See individual cost pages for detailed ranges and configuration options.

Factors That Affect Cost

Configuration (engine, transmission, axle ratio), options (sleeper vs day cab, aerial reach for bucket trucks, tank size for vac trucks), manufacturer, and region. New trucks have seen price increases from emissions requirements and supply chain. Used prices follow new market; age, mileage, and condition drive valuation. See Commercial Truck Depreciation for depreciation by type.

Using Cost Data for Financing

Lenders use equipment value to set advance rates (loan-to-value). Knowing typical costs helps you budget and negotiate. A $120k semi at 80% advance = $96k max loan; you need $24k down. Used equipment at 50% of new cost may qualify for 70–75% advance. See Typical Down Payments and Equipment Cost Guides for detailed pricing.

Typical Cost Range

Most vocational and over-the-road trucks fall in the $50,000–$250,000 range new. Specialty and municipal equipment can exceed $500,000. Used prices depend on age, mileage, and condition.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need to finance commercial trucks?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Cost affects loan amount.

How much down payment for commercial trucks?

Typically 10–30%. New trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit can reduce requirements.

Can startups finance commercial trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down. Used trucks reduce upfront cost.

How long are commercial truck loan terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 24–60 months. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can I get commercial truck financing?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks.

Can I finance used commercial trucks?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available. Lower cost means smaller loan amount. Terms typically 24–60 months.

What documentation do I need for truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details (invoice or listing).

What is the average cost of a semi truck?

New semi tractors: $120,000–$200,000. Used: $50,000–$120,000.

What is the average cost of a dump truck?

New dump trucks: $80,000–$150,000. Used: $45,000–$90,000.

What is the average cost of a bucket truck?

New bucket trucks: $80,000–$250,000. Used: $40,000–$120,000.
