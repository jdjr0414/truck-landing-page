Commercial Truck Operating Costs

Commercial truck operating costs include fuel, maintenance, insurance, tires, permits, and loan payments. Semi truck costs are often expressed per mile ($1.50–$2.50+). Vocational trucks may use different metrics. This page summarizes typical cost categories and ranges.

Quick Answer

Typical semi operating cost: $1.50–$2.50+ per mile. Main costs: fuel, maintenance, insurance, tires, permits, loan payment. Vocational trucks vary by use. See depreciation.

Common Industries & Cost Structure

Over-the-road trucking: per-mile costs. Vocational (dump, bucket, vac): often hourly or per-job. Fuel and maintenance are major variables. Loan payment is fixed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main operating costs for a semi truck?

Fuel, driver wages, maintenance, tires, insurance, permits, and loan/lease payments. Fuel is often the largest variable cost.

How much does it cost to operate a semi truck per mile?

Total operating cost often $1.50–$2.50+ per mile including fuel, maintenance, insurance, and fixed costs.

What are typical maintenance costs for a commercial truck?

$0.10–$0.15 per mile for routine maintenance. Major repairs add more.

How much does commercial truck insurance cost?

$8,000–$20,000+ annually for primary liability and physical damage.
