Average Semi Truck Down Payment

The average semi truck down payment typically falls in the 15–20% range. New tractors often require 10–20%; used 15–25%. Lower credit may need 25–35%. This page summarizes typical down payment percentages and example dollar amounts.

Common Questions

What credit score affects semi truck down payment?

Strong credit (720+) may qualify with $0 down. 650–719: 10–15%. Below 650: 20–35%. Down payments are risk-based.

What is the average down payment for a semi truck?

Industry average: 15–20%. New: 10–20%. Used: 15–25%.

Can startup owner-operators get financing with less down?

Startups typically need 20–30% down. Proof of carrier agreements can help. Used trucks reduce upfront cost.

How long are semi truck loan terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months. Terms are tied to equipment, not down payment.

How fast can I get approved with a low down payment?

Approval speed doesn't depend on down payment. Simple applications: 1–3 business days.

How much down for a used semi truck?

Used typically requires 15–25% down. Older or higher-mileage units may require more.

What documents affect down payment requirements?

Tax returns, bank statements, and P&L show revenue. Strong revenue and credit can reduce down payment.

How much is 20% down on a $150,000 semi?

$30,000. Loan amount would be $120,000.

Can businesses qualify with no down payment?

Yes. Strong credit and established businesses often qualify with $0 down. Down payments are risk-based, not mandatory.

Does a larger down payment lower my rate?

Often yes. Larger down payments can qualify for better APRs.
