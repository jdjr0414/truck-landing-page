Common Questions

How do I choose between truck types for my business?

Match the truck to your typical loads, routes, and required licensing. Compare payload, GVWR, fuel economy, and upfront cost against the work you will actually do.

Which commercial truck is cheapest to operate?

Lighter single-unit trucks such as box trucks and cargo vans generally have the lowest fuel and maintenance costs; heavy and specialized trucks cost more to run but earn more per job.

Can I finance more than one vehicle type at once?

Yes. Many lenders will structure a single facility or multiple loans across a mixed fleet, which can simplify payments as you scale.
