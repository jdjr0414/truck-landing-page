Common Questions

What credit score do I need for box truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Delivery revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a box truck?

Typically 10–30%. New box trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit can reduce requirements.

Can startup delivery companies get box truck financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer businesses. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are box truck loan terms?

New box trucks: 36–60 months. Used: 24–48 months depending on age and condition.

How fast can box truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks.

Can I finance a used box truck?

Yes. Used box truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 24–48 months.

What documentation do I need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details.

What do box trucks cost?

New: $35,000–$80,000. Used: $15,000–$50,000. Cost depends on size, refrigeration, and condition.
