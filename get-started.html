Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment. Higher-risk scenariosùstartups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding creditùmay require 20ù30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business typically receive the best termsùoften 10ù15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15ù25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20ù30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.
  • Companies rebuilding credit: Specialty lenders may work with 580ù650 scores; expect 15ù25% down.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need?

Many programs require 650+ for competitive terms. Some lenders work with 580ù650 with higher down payments.

How much down payment is required?

Typically 10ù30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with $0 down.

Can startups get truck financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20ù30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are truck loan terms?

New trucks: 60ù84 months. Used trucks: 24ù60 months depending on age and mileage.

How fast can I get approved?

Simple applications: 1ù3 business days. Complex deals: 1ù2 weeks. Approval typically takes 24ù72 hours for qualified applicants.

Can I finance used trucks?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically shorter than for new equipment.

What documentation do I need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details.

What do commercial trucks cost?

Semi trucks: $120,000ù$200,000. Dump trucks: $80,000ù$180,000. Bucket trucks: $90,000ù$250,000. Costs vary by type.
