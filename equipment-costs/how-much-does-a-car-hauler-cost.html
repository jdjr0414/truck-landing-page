How Much Does a Car Hauler Cost?

A new open auto transport trailer (7–10 car capacity) costs $35,000–$80,000. Enclosed 2-car trailers run $15,000–$40,000 new. Hotshot car hauler trailers (2–3 cars, gooseneck) cost $10,000–$25,000. A complete semi + open trailer setup runs $130,000–$280,000 new. Used open car haulers range from $15,000 to $50,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance do car haulers need?

Auto transport operators need commercial auto liability coverage (minimum $750,000 in most states, $1 million recommended), cargo insurance specifically for motor vehicle cargo (not standard freight cargo insurance), and physical damage coverage for the tractor and trailer. Auto transport cargo insurance is a specialized product — standard freight cargo policies typically exclude motor vehicle transport. Budget $1,000–$3,000/month for a complete auto transport insurance package.

Is the hotshot car hauling business profitable?

Hotshot car hauling (2–3 vehicles per load, pickup-towed) has lower startup costs than full semi-trailer operations but also lower revenue per load. A hotshot operator charging $600–$900 per vehicle on 2–3 vehicle loads generates $1,200–$2,700 per trip. With 3–4 trips per week, gross revenue is $3,600–$10,800 weekly before fuel, insurance, and loan payments. For owner-operators starting out, hotshot is a lower-barrier entry than a full Class A semi setup. See our hotshot truck cost guide for the towing vehicle.
