How Much Does a Food Truck Cost?

A new fully equipped food truck costs $75,000–$200,000. Used food trucks range from $30,000 for basic older units to $120,000+ for late-model well-equipped trucks. The chassis accounts for $30,000–$60,000 and the kitchen buildout — cooking equipment, refrigeration, ventilation, generator — accounts for $40,000–$140,000 depending on the menu and finish level.

Key Takeaways

  • New fully equipped food truck: $75,000 – $200,000
  • Kitchen buildout alone: $40,000 – $140,000
  • Used food trucks: $30,000 – $120,000
  • Most food trucks do not require a CDL (under 26,001 lbs GVWR)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a commissary kitchen and do I need one?

Most cities require food trucks to operate out of a licensed commissary kitchen — a commercial kitchen where the truck prepper and cleans equipment. The commissary kitchen is not the truck itself; it's a separate facility. Commissary kitchen rental runs $400–$1,200/month depending on location and usage hours. When evaluating a food truck market, confirm the commissary requirement and identify available facilities before committing.

Is buying a used food truck a good idea?

Used food trucks can be a good value if inspected carefully. Key items to check: generator condition and hours (replacement runs $3,000–$8,000), ventilation hood suppression system certification (inspection and recertification may be required), propane line inspection, refrigeration condition, and chassis mechanical condition. Health code compliance for the kitchen layout in your specific city is also critical — equipment that passed inspection in another jurisdiction may not pass locally.
