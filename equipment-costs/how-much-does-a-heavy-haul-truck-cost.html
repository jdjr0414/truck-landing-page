How Much Does a Heavy Haul Truck Cost?

A new Class 8 heavy haul tractor costs $175,000–$350,000+ depending on horsepower, configuration, and manufacturer. Used heavy haul tractors range from $80,000 to $200,000 for well-maintained units. The total heavy haul setup — tractor plus lowboy or RGN trailer — typically runs $250,000–$500,000+ new and $120,000–$280,000 used.

Key Takeaways

  • New heavy haul tractor (Class 8, high-HP): $175,000 – $350,000+
  • New lowboy / RGN trailer: $50,000 – $200,000+
  • Class A CDL required; overdimensional loads require state permits
  • Used heavy haul tractors: $80,000 – $200,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What loads does heavy haul trucking transport?

Heavy haul trucking moves loads that exceed standard legal weight (80,000 lbs gross) or standard legal dimensions (13.6 ft height, 8.5 ft width, 53 ft length in most states). Common loads include construction equipment (excavators, cranes, bulldozers), industrial machinery, transformers and electrical equipment, modular building sections, wind turbine components, and oversized manufacturing equipment.

Do I need pilot cars for heavy haul moves?

Pilot car requirements are state-specific and based on load dimensions. Most states require one front pilot car for loads over 14 ft wide or 100 ft long; rear pilot cars are required for loads over certain widths in many states. Some states also require State Police escorts for very large or heavy loads. The permit application process will specify escort requirements for each state on the route.
