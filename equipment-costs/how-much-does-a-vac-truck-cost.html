Common Questions

How much does a new vac truck cost?

New vac trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on tank capacity and configuration. Hydro excavation units can exceed $300,000.

How much does a used vac truck cost?

Used vac trucks range from $40,000 to $150,000 depending on age, tank capacity, and condition.

What credit score do I need for vac truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Contract revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a vac truck?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and environmental contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup environmental companies finance vac trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer businesses. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are vac truck loan terms?

New vac trucks: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and condition. Hydro excavation units may qualify for longer terms.

How fast can vac truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used vac truck?

Yes. Used vac truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do I need for vac truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of contracts or revenue, and equipment details. Disposal permits may be required.

What affects vac truck pricing?

Tank capacity, vacuum specs, chassis, and configuration (hydro excavation vs septic vs industrial).
