How Much Does an Ambulance Cost?

Ambulance costs vary by type (Type I, II, III) and medical equipment. New ambulances range from $150,000 to $280,000. Used ambulances range from $80,000 to $180,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for EMS and medical transport.

Key Takeaways

  • New ambulances typically cost $150,000–$280,000
  • Used ambulances range from $80,000–$180,000
  • Type I (conventional cab) and Type III (cutaway) are most common

Factors That Affect Cost

Type (I, II, III) and chassis affect base price. Type II (van-based) is typically lower cost. Type I (conventional cab) and Type III (cutaway) offer more interior space and command higher prices. Medical equipment (defibrillators, monitors, stretchers, oxygen) adds cost. Custom builds and specialty configurations increase price. Used ambulances from municipal or private fleet turnover are common in the secondary market.

Financing Considerations

Ambulance financing is widely available. Down payment typically 15–25% for new, 20–30% for used. Terms: 48–72 months new, 36–60 months used. EMS and medical transport companies with contract revenue receive favorable terms. See Ambulance Financing and Typical Down Payments.

How much does a used ambulance cost?

Do I need a CDL for an ambulance?

What is Type I vs Type III ambulance?

Type I has a conventional truck cab; Type III has a cutaway van chassis. Both offer full patient compartment. Type II is van-based with lower profile.

Can I finance an ambulance?

Yes. Ambulance financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–25%.

Do I need a CDL for an ambulance?

Depends on GVWR and state. Many ambulances exceed 26,000 lbs GVWR and may require Class B CDL. Check your state regulations.
