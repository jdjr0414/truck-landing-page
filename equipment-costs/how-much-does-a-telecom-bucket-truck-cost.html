How Much Does a Telecom Bucket Truck Cost?

A new telecom bucket truck (insulated aerial lift) costs $90,000–$200,000 for a standard 40–55 ft unit. Used telecom bucket trucks range from $40,000 for older high-mileage units to $110,000+ for late-model well-maintained trucks. Telecom bucket trucks are distinct from standard bucket trucks — they require insulated booms rated for proximity to energized lines and are configured specifically for cable, fiber, and telephone pole work.

Key Takeaways

  • New telecom aerial truck (40–55 ft): $90,000 – $200,000
  • Insulated boom required for work near energized lines
  • Many units do not require a CDL (Class 4–5 chassis)
  • Used telecom bucket trucks: $40,000 – $110,000

Frequently Asked Questions

How often must the insulation on a telecom bucket truck be tested?

OSHA and industry standards generally require insulation testing of aerial devices used near energized lines annually. Testing is performed by certified aerial device testing facilities and verifies the boom's dielectric rating. An untested boom that has exceeded its test interval cannot legally be used near energized conductors. Testing typically costs $500–$1,500 depending on the device and testing facility.

Can I use a telecom bucket truck for tree work?

Technically yes, but it's not ideal. Telecom aerial trucks are designed for lighter loads and single-technician work — they lack the platform weight capacity and durability needed for the abusive loads of tree work (chainsaw vibration, brush weight, limb removal). Using a telecom aerial for tree work accelerates wear on the boom and hydraulic components. A tree service bucket truck is better suited.
