How Much Does a Charter Bus Cost?

Charter bus costs vary by size and amenities. New charter buses range from $300,000 to $550,000. Used charter buses range from $100,000 to $350,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for group transportation.

Key Takeaways

  • New charter buses typically cost $300,000–$550,000
  • Used charter buses range from $100,000–$350,000
  • Class A or B CDL with passenger endorsement typically required

Factors Affecting Charter Bus Cost

Seating capacity (35–56 passengers), manufacturer (MCI, Prevost, Van Hool, etc.), powertrain (diesel vs alternative fuel), and amenities (WiFi, lavatory, entertainment). Full-size coaches cost more than midsize. Used buses depreciate; 5–10 year old units often sell at 40–60% of new. See How Much Does a Coach Bus Cost for comparison.

Licensing and Insurance

Charter bus operators typically need Class A or B CDL with passenger endorsement. Insurance requirements vary; many carriers require $5M+ liability for charter work. Lenders verify CDL and insurance before funding. See Commercial Truck License Requirements.

Common Questions

How much does a new charter bus cost?

New charter buses range from $300,000 to $550,000 depending on size and amenities.

How much does a used charter bus cost?

Used charter buses range from $100,000 to $350,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition.

Charter bus vs coach bus cost?

Charter buses and coach buses overlap in price. Coach buses are typically full-size motorcoaches; charter buses may include smaller transit-style units.

Can I finance a charter bus?

Yes. Charter bus financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–30%.

What CDL do I need for a charter bus?

Class A or Class B CDL with passenger endorsement is typically required for charter bus operation.
