How Much Does a Limousine Cost?

Limousine costs vary by base vehicle and stretch configuration. New limousines range from $80,000 to $200,000+. Used limousines range from $40,000 to $120,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for luxury transportation.

Key Takeaways

  • New limousines typically cost $80,000–$200,000+
  • Used limousines range from $40,000–$120,000
  • Financing terms commonly range from 36–60 months

Factors Affecting Limousine Cost

Base vehicle (Lincoln, Cadillac, Chevrolet), stretch length (standard vs extended), interior amenities (leather, entertainment, bar), and mileage for used units all affect price. Premium brands and longer stretches command higher prices. Regional demand and seasonal factors can influence used market values.

New vs Used Comparison

New limousines offer full warranty and latest amenities but cost $80k–$200k+. Used units ($40k–$120k) reduce upfront cost but may need interior refresh or mechanical work. Financing terms for used limousines are typically 24–48 months vs 36–60 months for new. See New vs Used Truck Financing for general guidance.

Common Questions

How much does a new limousine cost?

New limousines range from $80,000 to $200,000+ depending on base vehicle and stretch configuration.

How much does a used limousine cost?

Used limousines range from $40,000 to $120,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition.

Can I finance a limousine?

Yes. Limousine financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–25%.

Do I need a CDL for a limousine?

Most stretch limousines are under 26,000 lbs GVWR and do not require a CDL. Check your state and vehicle specs.

What affects limousine resale value?

Mileage, maintenance history, interior condition, and base vehicle brand affect resale value.
