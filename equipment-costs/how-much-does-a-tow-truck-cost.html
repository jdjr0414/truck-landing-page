How Much Does a Tow Truck Cost?

Tow truck costs vary by type (flatbed, wheel-lift, integrated) and capacity. New tow trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+. Heavy-duty recovery trucks can exceed $200,000. Used tow trucks range from $30,000 to $90,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new tow truck cost?

New tow trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+ depending on type. Heavy-duty recovery trucks can exceed $200,000.

How much does a used tow truck cost?

Used tow trucks range from $30,000 to $90,000 depending on age, type, and condition.

Flatbed vs wheel-lift cost?

Flatbed tow trucks typically cost more than wheel-lift. Integrated tow trucks (boom + wheel-lift) command premium prices.

Can I finance a tow truck?

Yes. Tow truck financing is widely available. Down payments typically run 10–30%.
