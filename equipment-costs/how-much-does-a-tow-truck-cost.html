How Much Does a Tow Truck Cost?

Tow truck costs vary by type (flatbed, wheel-lift, integrated, rotator) and capacity. New tow trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+. Rotator tow trucks (360° rotating boom) cost $800,000–$1.7M new. Heavy-duty recovery trucks can exceed $200,000. Used tow trucks range from $30,000 to $90,000; used rotators $300,000–$900,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • Tow trucks typically cost between $60 and $150
  • Financing terms commonly range from 36-60 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

Common Questions

How much does a new tow truck cost?

New tow trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+ depending on type. Heavy-duty recovery trucks can exceed $200,000.

How much does a used tow truck cost?

Used tow trucks range from $30,000 to $90,000 depending on age, type, and condition.

What credit score do I need for tow truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Towing revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a tow truck?

Typically 10–30%. New tow trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and AAA contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup towing companies finance tow trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer towing businesses. Expect 20–30% down and proof of revenue or AAA contracts.

How long are tow truck loan terms?

New tow trucks: 36–60 months. Used: 24–48 months depending on age and condition. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can tow truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used tow truck?

Yes. Used tow truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 24–48 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do I need for tow truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of revenue or AAA contracts, and equipment details. CDL may be required for some units.

Flatbed vs wheel-lift cost?

Flatbed tow trucks typically cost more than wheel-lift. Integrated tow trucks (boom + wheel-lift) command premium prices. Rotator tow trucks are the most expensive—$800k–$1.7M new—for heavy semi and equipment recovery.

How much does a rotator tow truck cost?

Rotator tow trucks cost $800,000–$1,700,000 new and $300,000–$900,000 used. They feature a 360° rotating boom for heavy semi and equipment recovery. Operators typically need Class A CDL and crane certification (NCCCO, WreckMaster).
