Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new crane truck cost?

New crane trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on crane capacity (tonnage) and reach.

How much does a used crane truck cost?

Used crane trucks range from $40,000 to $150,000 depending on age, crane capacity, and condition.

What affects crane truck pricing?

Crane capacity (tonnage), reach, chassis, and condition. Knuckle-boom and articulated cranes vary in price.

Can I finance a crane truck?

Yes. Crane truck financing is widely available. Down payments typically run 10–30%.
