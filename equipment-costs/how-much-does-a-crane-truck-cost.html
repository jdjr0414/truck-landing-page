How Much Does a Crane Truck Cost?

Crane truck costs vary by crane capacity, reach, and chassis. New crane trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+. Used crane trucks range from $40,000 to $150,000. Higher capacity cranes command premium prices. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • Crane trucks typically cost between $80 and $250
  • Financing terms commonly range from 48-72 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

Common Questions

How much does a new crane truck cost?

New crane trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on crane capacity (tonnage) and reach.

How much does a used crane truck cost?

Used crane trucks range from $40,000 to $150,000 depending on age, crane capacity, and condition.

What credit score do I need for crane truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Contract revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a crane truck?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and construction contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup contractors finance crane trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer contractors. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are crane truck loan terms?

New crane trucks: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and crane condition. Crane capacity affects terms.

How fast can crane truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used crane truck?

Yes. Used crane truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Crane inspections may be required.

What documentation do I need for crane truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of contracts, and equipment details. Crane certifications may be required.

What affects crane truck pricing?

Crane capacity (tonnage), reach, chassis, and condition. Knuckle-boom and articulated cranes vary in price.
