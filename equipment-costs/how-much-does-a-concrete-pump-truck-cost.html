How Much Does a Concrete Pump Truck Cost?

A new 28-meter boom pump truck costs $350,000–$600,000. New 36-meter boom pumps run $550,000–$800,000. Large 47-meter+ units cost $800,000–$1,200,000+. Trailer-mounted line pumps cost $80,000–$180,000 new. Used boom pumps range from $150,000–$500,000 depending on boom length, meter count, and age.

Key Takeaways

  • New 28-meter boom pump: $350,000 – $600,000
  • New trailer line pump: $80,000 – $180,000
  • Used boom pumps: $150,000 – $500,000
  • Billing rate: $150 – $250/hr; 4-hr minimum typical

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ACPA certification and is it required?

The American Concrete Pumping Association (ACPA) offers Concrete Pump Operator certification. Most commercial general contractors require ACPA-certified operators on their projects, and it is increasingly written into project specifications. Certification requires passing a written exam and demonstrating practical operation competency. ACPA certification is distinct from CDL requirements and applies specifically to concrete pump operation.

How is meter count used to evaluate used pump value?

Meter count (total cubic meters pumped) is the primary wear indicator for concrete pumps — analogous to mileage on a truck. The pump's concrete-contact components (pistons, cylinders, wear plates, S-valve or rock valve) degrade with use. A 5-year-old pump with 50,000 meters pumped is in much better condition than a 3-year-old pump with 150,000 meters. Ask for the meter count and maintenance history when evaluating any used pump. Wear parts replacement can run $15,000–$80,000 depending on the components needing service.
