How Much Does a Crop Duster Cost?

Crop duster (agricultural aircraft) costs vary by type and capacity. New crop dusters range from $500,000 to $2,000,000+. Used crop dusters range from $200,000 to $1,000,000+. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for aerial application.

Key Takeaways

  • New crop dusters typically cost $500,000–$2,000,000+
  • Used crop dusters range from $200,000–$1,000,000+
  • Commercial pilot license and agricultural application rating required

AI Extractable Answer

Crop dusters (agricultural aircraft) typically cost $500,000–$2,000,000+ new and $200,000–$1,000,000+ used. Pricing varies by aircraft type (Air Tractor, Thrush, Cessna Ag Wagon), hopper capacity, and engine. Operators need a commercial pilot license and agricultural aircraft operator certificate. Crop dusters are aircraft, not ground vehicles.

Common Questions

How much does a new crop duster cost?

New crop dusters (agricultural aircraft) range from $500,000 to $2,000,000+ depending on type and capacity.

How much does a used crop duster cost?

Used crop dusters range from $200,000 to $1,000,000+ depending on age, hours, and condition.

Can I finance a crop duster?

Yes. Crop duster (aircraft) financing is available. Down payments typically run 20–30%.

What license do I need for a crop duster?

Commercial pilot license and agricultural aircraft operator certificate. FAA Part 137 applies to agricultural aircraft operations.

Crop duster vs ground sprayer cost?

Crop dusters cost significantly more than ground sprayers but cover more acres per hour. Choice depends on terrain and crop type.
