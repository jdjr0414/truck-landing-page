How Much Does a Commercial Pickup Truck Cost?

A new 3/4-ton commercial pickup (F-250, Ram 2500, Silverado 2500) costs $48,000–$75,000. A new 1-ton dually (F-350, Ram 3500, Silverado 3500) runs $52,000–$82,000. Super-duty Class 4–5 trucks (F-450, Ram 4500) run $58,000–$90,000. Used commercial pickups range from $25,000 for older units to $55,000+ for low-mileage late models. No CDL required for trucks under 26,001 lbs GVWR.

Key Takeaways

  • New 3/4-ton pickup (F-250, Ram 2500): $48,000 – $75,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best pickup truck for contractors?

For most contractors, the Ford F-250 or F-350 Super Duty with the 6.2L gas engine is the best all-around choice: competitive price, best-in-class payload, available upfit options, and the widest service network in the U.S. Contractors who do significant highway mileage or heavy trailer towing often prefer the 6.7L Power Stroke diesel for fuel savings and torque. Ram 2500/3500 with the Cummins diesel is also popular and highly regarded for towing. The choice often comes down to brand loyalty and local dealer relationships.

What upfits are most common on contractor pickups?

Contractor pickups are commonly upfitted with service bodies (aluminum utility beds replacing the stock bed), headache racks, ladder racks, toolboxes (crossbed, side mount, and underbody), and fifth wheel hitches. Service body upfits from brands like Reading, Knapheide, and Adrian Steel add $8,000–$20,000 to the base truck cost but dramatically improve tool organization and payload efficiency.
