How Much Does a Sprinter Van Cost?

Sprinter van costs vary by make (Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, Ford Transit, Ram ProMaster) and configuration. New sprinter-style vans range from $40,000 to $75,000. Used sprinter vans range from $25,000 to $55,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • New sprinter vans typically cost $40,000–$75,000
  • Used sprinter vans range from $25,000–$55,000
  • Financing terms commonly range from 36–60 months

Common Questions

How much does a new sprinter van cost?

New sprinter-style vans range from $40,000 to $75,000 depending on make, model, and configuration.

How much does a used sprinter van cost?

Used sprinter vans range from $25,000 to $55,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition.

Sprinter vs cargo van cost?

Sprinter vans typically cost more than traditional cargo vans due to higher roof, better payload, and premium branding.

Can I finance a sprinter van?

Yes. Sprinter van financing is widely available. Down payments typically run 10–25%.

Do I need a CDL for a sprinter van?

Most sprinter vans are under 26,000 lbs GVWR and do not require a CDL. Check your state and vehicle specs.
