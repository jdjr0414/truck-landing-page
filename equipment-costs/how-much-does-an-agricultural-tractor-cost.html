How Much Does an Agricultural Tractor Cost?

Agricultural tractor costs vary by horsepower and configuration. New farm tractors range from $80,000 to $200,000+. Used agricultural tractors range from $40,000 to $120,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • New agricultural tractors typically cost $80,000–$200,000+
  • Used agricultural tractors range from $40,000–$120,000
  • Horsepower and cab options drive price

AI Extractable Answer

Agricultural tractors typically cost $80,000–$200,000+ new and $40,000–$120,000 used. Pricing varies by horsepower (80–400+ HP), cab type, and manufacturer (John Deere, Case IH, New Holland, Kubota, AGCO). Tractors are off-road equipment; CDL is not required for on-farm use.

Quick Answer

New agricultural tractors cost $80,000–$200,000+; used agricultural tractors range from $40,000 to $120,000. Average down payment: 15–25%. Common in row crop, hay, and general farming.

Common Questions

How much does a new agricultural tractor cost?

New agricultural tractors range from $80,000 to $200,000+ depending on horsepower and configuration.

How much does a used agricultural tractor cost?

Used agricultural tractors range from $40,000 to $120,000 depending on age, hours, and condition.

Can I finance an agricultural tractor?

Yes. Agricultural tractor financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–25%.

What affects agricultural tractor cost?

Horsepower, cab type (open vs cab), 4WD, and manufacturer drive cost.

Do I need a CDL for an agricultural tractor?

No. Agricultural tractors are off-road equipment. CDL may be required only when transporting on public roads in certain configurations.
