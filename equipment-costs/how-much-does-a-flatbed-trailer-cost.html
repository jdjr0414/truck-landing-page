How Much Does a Flatbed Trailer Cost?

Flatbed trailer costs vary by length, configuration, and condition. New standard flatbeds range from $20,000 to $45,000. Used flatbeds run $10,000 to $30,000. Hotshot trailers (smaller) cost less; step-deck and lowboy trailers cost more. This guide breaks down pricing by type and how to finance. See trailer financing for trucking and flatbed truck financing.

Key Takeaways

  • New flatbeds: $20,000–$45,000; used: $10,000–$30,000
  • Hotshot trailers (40') typically $15,000–$35,000 new
  • Trailer financing terms typically 36–60 months

What Affects Flatbed Trailer Pricing

Length (40', 48', 53'), deck material (wood vs aluminum), axle count, and features (stake pockets, rub rails, ramps). Aluminum decks cost more but weigh less. Used pricing depends on age, mileage, deck condition, and tire life. See best trailer for hotshot for hotshot-specific options.

Financing a Flatbed Trailer

Flatbed trailer financing is widely available. Terms typically 36–60 months. Down payment 10–20% common. Can finance trailer separately from tractor. See trailer financing for trucking for details.

Common Questions

How much does a new flatbed trailer cost?

New flatbeds: $20,000–$45,000 depending on length and configuration. Standard 48'–53': $25,000–$40,000.

How much does a used flatbed trailer cost?

Used: $10,000–$30,000. 3–5 year old units: $15,000–$25,000 depending on condition.

Can I finance a flatbed trailer?

Yes. Terms typically 36–60 months. See trailer financing for trucking.
