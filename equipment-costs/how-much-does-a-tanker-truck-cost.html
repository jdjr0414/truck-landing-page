How Much Does a Tanker Truck Cost?

Tanker truck costs vary significantly by type — a used water tanker starts around $40,000, while a new stainless steel food-grade or chemical tanker can exceed $300,000. This guide covers pricing by tanker type and capacity, plus CDL requirements, operating costs, and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • Fuel tankers: $100,000–$200,000 new, $50,000–$120,000 used
  • Water tankers: $80,000–$150,000 new, $40,000–$90,000 used
  • Most tanker drivers need a CDL with Tank (N) endorsement
  • Hazmat tanker drivers also need Hazmat (H) endorsement

What Affects Tanker Truck Pricing

Tank material is the largest price driver after chassis. Aluminum tanks are the most common for fuel and water. Stainless steel tanks — required for food-grade, dairy, and some chemical applications — add $20,000–$80,000 to the cost versus aluminum. Carbon steel tanks are sometimes used for asphalt and industrial applications.

Tank capacity typically ranges from 1,500 to 10,000 gallons for a single-unit tanker. Larger capacity requires a heavier chassis and a Class A CDL for tractor-trailer configurations.

Hazmat compliance equipment — vapor recovery systems, emergency shutoffs, bonding cables, placards — adds $5,000–$15,000 to fuel and chemical tankers versus non-hazmat water tankers.

Pumping and metering equipment — fuel delivery pumps, flow meters, hose reels — can add $10,000–$30,000 to fuel delivery tankers used for site fueling operations.

CDL and Endorsement Requirements

Most commercial tanker operations require a Class A CDL (combination vehicles over 26,001 lbs GVWR with a towed trailer over 10,000 lbs) or Class B CDL (single vehicle over 26,001 lbs GVWR).

The Tank Vehicle (N) endorsement is required for drivers of vehicles with tanks of 1,000 gallons or more, or that are designed to transport liquids or gases. This endorsement requires a written knowledge test.

The Hazardous Materials (H) endorsement is additionally required for drivers of vehicles transporting hazardous materials requiring placarding — including most petroleum products, chemicals, and compressed gases. The H endorsement requires a knowledge test, background check, and TSA security threat assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a fuel tanker truck cost?

A new fuel tanker truck costs $100,000–$200,000 depending on chassis, tank capacity (typically 5,000–10,000 gallons for a single-unit), and pumping/metering equipment. Used fuel tankers in serviceable condition run $50,000–$120,000.

Can I use a tanker truck for both water and fuel delivery?

No. Fuel and water tanks use different materials and construction. An aluminum fuel tank cannot be safely used for potable water delivery, and a water tank is not safe for fuel without proper cleaning, testing, and compliance review. Most states prohibit repurposing tanks without certified cleaning and inspection.

How do I finance a tanker truck?

Tanker truck financing is available from equipment lenders specializing in commercial trucks. The tank and chassis typically serve as collateral. Hazmat-configured tankers may have slightly higher insurance requirements affecting lender risk assessment. See tanker truck financing for options.
