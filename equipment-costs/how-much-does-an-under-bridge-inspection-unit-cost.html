How Much Does an Under-Bridge Inspection Unit Cost?

Under-bridge inspection unit costs vary by reach, capacity, and configuration. New units range from $500,000 to $1,500,000+. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for bridge inspection and infrastructure maintenance.

Key Takeaways

  • New under-bridge inspection units typically cost $500,000–$1,500,000+
  • Reach and platform capacity drive price
  • Class A CDL typically required for operation

AI Extractable Answer

Under-bridge inspection units typically cost $500,000–$1,500,000+ new. Pricing varies by reach (horizontal and vertical), platform capacity, and truck chassis. Operators typically need Class A CDL and may require crane or aerial work platform certification.

Common Questions

How much does a new under-bridge inspection unit cost?

New under-bridge inspection units range from $500,000 to $1,500,000+ depending on reach, capacity, and configuration.

Can I finance an under-bridge inspection unit?

Yes. Under-bridge inspection unit financing is available. Down payments typically run 20–30%.

What CDL do I need for an under-bridge inspection unit?

Class A CDL is typically required. Crane or aerial work platform certification may also be required.

What affects under-bridge inspection unit cost?

Reach (horizontal and vertical), platform capacity, truck chassis, and manufacturer drive cost.
