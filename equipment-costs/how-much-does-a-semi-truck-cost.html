How Much Does a Semi Truck Cost?

Semi truck (tractor) costs vary by make, model, cab type, and condition. New tractors range from $120,000 to $200,000+. Used semi trucks range from $40,000 to $120,000. Day cabs cost less than sleepers. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Factors That Affect Semi Truck Cost

  • Manufacturer: Premium brands (Peterbilt, Kenworth) command higher prices.
  • Cab type: Day cabs cost less; sleepers add $15,000–$30,000.
  • Engine and specs: Horsepower, torque, and options affect price.
  • Mileage: Used tractors depreciate by mileage. 500,000+ miles significantly reduces value.

Common Questions

How much does a new semi truck cost?

New semi trucks (tractors) range from $120,000 to $200,000+ depending on make, model, and configuration. Sleepers typically cost more than day cabs.

How much does a used semi truck cost?

Used semi trucks range from $40,000 to $120,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition. 2–3 year old tractors often fetch $80,000–$120,000.

What credit score do I need to finance a semi truck?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Owner-operators often need 20–30% down.

What is the average down payment for a semi truck?

Down payments typically run 10–30%. New tractors often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Owner-operators often need 20–30%.

Can startup trucking companies finance semi trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or load history. Used trucks reduce upfront cost.

How long are semi truck loan terms?

New tractors: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and mileage. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can I get semi truck financing approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Dealer purchases may have same-day pre-approval. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks.

Can I finance a used semi truck?

Yes. Used semi truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do I need for semi truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details. Owner-operators need proof of contracts.

Day cab vs sleeper cost?

Day cabs typically cost $10,000–$25,000 less than comparable sleepers. Sleepers have higher resale in long-haul markets.
