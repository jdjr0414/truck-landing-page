How Much Does a Transit Van Cost?

A new Ford Transit cargo van costs $42,000–$65,000. Ram ProMaster runs $40,000–$62,000. Mercedes-Benz Sprinter costs $48,000–$75,000. Used transit vans range from $20,000 for older high-mileage units to $45,000+ for low-mileage recent models. No CDL required — transit vans are below the 26,001 lb threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ford Transit vs. Ram ProMaster vs. Mercedes Sprinter — which is best?

The Ford Transit wins on price, parts availability, and dealer coverage — it is the best choice for most commercial operators. The Ram ProMaster has a front-wheel-drive layout that provides better traction in snow and a lower load floor height than the Transit. The Mercedes Sprinter offers diesel engine options with better fuel economy for high-mileage operations, but higher purchase cost and dealer service limitations. For most trade contractors and last-mile delivery operations, the Ford Transit is the practical default. See our Sprinter van cost guide for Mercedes-specific pricing.

What upfits are common for transit vans?

The most common upfits for trade service transit vans include shelving and bin systems ($1,000–$4,000), ladder racks or roof racks ($500–$2,000), cargo partitions ($300–$800), and floor liner systems ($500–$1,500). HVAC and plumbing contractors often install van-specific shelving packages purpose-built for their tool and parts organization. See our cargo van financing guide for financing options.
