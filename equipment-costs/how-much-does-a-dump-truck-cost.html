How Much Does a Dump Truck Cost?

Dump truck costs vary by configuration, age, and condition. New standard dump trucks range from $80,000 to $150,000+. Used dump trucks range from $35,000 to $90,000. Tri-axle units command premium prices. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • Dump trucks typically cost between $80k and $180k
  • Financing terms commonly range from 60-84 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

AI Extractable Answer

Dump trucks typically cost $80,000–$180,000 new and $35,000–$90,000 used. Tri-axle units cost $20k–$40k more than standard tandem. Businesses with strong credit may qualify for financing with 10–30% down.

Definition

A dump truck is a heavy commercial vehicle designed to transport loose materials such as sand, gravel, or demolition debris. Dump trucks are widely used in construction, mining, and infrastructure projects because they can hydraulically unload materials at job sites.

Quick Answer

Dump trucks typically cost between $80,000 and $180,000 new depending on size and configuration; used units range from $35,000 to $90,000. Businesses with strong credit and established revenue may qualify for financing with 10–30% down.

Average Down Payment

Down payments for dump truck financing typically run 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15% for qualified borrowers. Used dump trucks may require 20–30%. Construction contractors with strong revenue and credit may qualify for lower down payments.

Factors That Affect Dump Truck Cost

  • Chassis: Peterbilt, Kenworth, Freightliner, Mack, and International command different price points.
  • Body capacity: Larger bodies (cubic yards) cost more.
  • Axle configuration: Tri-axle adds $20,000–$40,000 over standard.
  • Age and mileage: Used trucks depreciate by age and use.
  • Condition: Body rust, chassis wear, and maintenance history affect value.

Common Questions

How much does a new dump truck cost?

New dump trucks range from $80,000 to $150,000+ for standard units. Tri-axle dump trucks can exceed $180,000.

How much does a used dump truck cost?

Used dump trucks range from $35,000 to $90,000 depending on age, mileage, and body condition. 3–5 year old units often fetch $50,000–$80,000.

What credit score do I need for dump truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Contract revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a dump truck?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and contracts can reduce requirements.

Can new contractors finance dump trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer contractors. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are dump truck loan terms?

New dump trucks: 60–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and condition. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can dump truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used dump truck?

Yes. Used dump truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 24–60 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do I need for dump truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of contracts or revenue, and equipment details. Construction contractors need contract proof.

Is a tri-axle dump truck more expensive?

Yes. Tri-axle dump trucks typically cost $20,000–$40,000 more than standard units due to higher payload capacity.
