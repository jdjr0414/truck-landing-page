How Much Does an Agricultural Sprayer Cost?

Agricultural sprayer costs vary by type (self-propelled vs pull-type) and tank size. New sprayers range from $150,000 to $400,000+. Used agricultural sprayers range from $80,000 to $250,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • New agricultural sprayers typically cost $150,000–$400,000+
  • Used agricultural sprayers range from $80,000–$250,000
  • Self-propelled units cost more than pull-type

AI Extractable Answer

Agricultural sprayers typically cost $150,000–$400,000+ new and $80,000–$250,000 used. Self-propelled sprayers cost more than pull-type. Pricing varies by tank capacity, boom width, and manufacturer (John Deere, Case IH, AGCO, Hagie). Sprayers are off-road equipment; CDL is not required for on-farm use.

Quick Answer

New agricultural sprayers cost $150,000–$400,000+; used agricultural sprayers range from $80,000 to $250,000. Average down payment: 15–25%. Common in row crop farming and custom application.

Common Questions

How much does a new agricultural sprayer cost?

New agricultural sprayers range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on type (self-propelled vs pull-type) and tank size.

How much does a used agricultural sprayer cost?

Used agricultural sprayers range from $80,000 to $250,000 depending on age, hours, and condition.

Self-propelled vs pull-type sprayer cost?

Self-propelled sprayers cost significantly more than pull-type sprayers due to dedicated chassis and cab.

Can I finance an agricultural sprayer?

Yes. Agricultural sprayer financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–25%.

Do I need a CDL for an agricultural sprayer?

No. Agricultural sprayers are off-road equipment. CDL may be required only when transporting on public roads.
