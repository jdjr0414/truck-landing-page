How Much Does a Refrigerated Trailer Cost?

A new 53-ft refrigerated trailer (reefer) costs $60,000–$100,000 including the refrigeration unit. Used reefer trailers range from $20,000 for older units to $65,000 for late-model trailers with well-maintained reefer units. This guide covers pricing by size, refrigeration unit costs, and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • New 53-ft reefer trailer: $60,000–$100,000
  • Refrigeration unit alone: $20,000–$40,000 new
  • Used 53-ft reefer in good condition: $35,000–$65,000
  • Reefer unit condition is the most important used buying factor

What to Check When Buying a Used Reefer

The condition of the refrigeration unit is the single most important factor when evaluating a used reefer trailer. A cheap trailer with a failing or high-hour reefer unit can cost more in repairs and downtime than a more expensive trailer with a well-maintained unit.

Key checks: hours on the reefer unit (most units are good for 25,000–35,000 hours with regular maintenance), service history from a Thermo King or Carrier dealer, recent pre-trip inspection results, and any history of refrigerant leaks.

The trailer insulation degrades over time — older trailers may not maintain temperature as efficiently, affecting fuel consumption and product quality on long hauls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Class A CDL cover refrigerated trailer operation?

Yes. A Class A CDL covers combination vehicles — tractor plus trailer — over 26,001 lbs. No additional endorsement is required specifically for reefer trailers unless the cargo is hazardous materials.

Can I lease a reefer trailer instead of buying?

Yes. Trailer leasing from trailer rental companies is common in refrigerated freight. Leasing trades ownership costs for flexibility. Owner-operators building a regular lane or customer base typically prefer ownership or financing for the equity and tax benefits.
