How Much Does a Coach Bus Cost?

Coach bus (motorcoach) costs vary by manufacturer and amenities. New coach buses range from $400,000 to $600,000+. Used coach buses range from $150,000 to $400,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for intercity and tour service.

Key Takeaways

  • New coach buses typically cost $400,000–$600,000+
  • Used coach buses range from $150,000–$400,000
  • Class A or B CDL typically required for operation

Common Questions

How much does a new coach bus cost?

New coach buses (motorcoaches) range from $400,000 to $600,000+ depending on manufacturer and amenities.

How much does a used coach bus cost?

Used coach buses range from $150,000 to $400,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition.

Coach bus vs charter bus cost?

Coach buses (motorcoaches) and charter buses overlap in price. Coach buses are typically full-size intercity units; charter buses may include smaller transit-style units.

Can I finance a coach bus?

Yes. Coach bus financing is available. Down payments typically run 15–30%.

What CDL do I need for a coach bus?

Class A or Class B CDL with passenger endorsement is typically required for coach bus operation.
