How Much Does a Fire Truck Cost?

Fire truck costs vary by type (pumper, aerial, ladder, rescue). New pumpers range from $300,000 to $600,000. Aerials and ladder trucks can exceed $1M. Used fire apparatus ranges from $100,000 to $500,000+. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Common Questions

How much does a new fire truck cost?

New fire trucks range from $300,000 to $1M+ depending on type. Pumpers: $300,000–$600,000. Aerials and ladder trucks: $800,000–$1.5M+.

How much does a used fire truck cost?

Used fire trucks range from $100,000 to $500,000+ depending on type, age, and condition.

What credit or rating do I need for fire truck financing?

Municipal buyers use municipal credit ratings. Industrial and volunteer departments may use commercial financing with 650+ personal/business credit.

How much down payment for a fire truck?

Municipal structures vary; lease-purchase may require minimal upfront. Commercial: typically 10–25%. Procurement processes affect structure.

Can volunteer fire departments finance fire trucks?

Yes. Volunteer and industrial fire departments can finance. May require 501(c)(3) documentation or municipal support. Terms may extend 10–15 years.

How long are fire truck loan terms?

Fire trucks often qualify for 10–15 year terms due to long useful life. Pumpers and aerials have extended lifespans. Municipal structures may vary.

How fast can fire truck financing be approved?

Municipal procurement: weeks to months. Commercial/industrial: 1–3 weeks. Bid processes and council approval extend municipal timeline.

Can I finance a used fire truck?

Yes. Used fire truck financing is available. Terms are shorter than for new. Specialized appraisals may be required. Common for volunteer departments.

What documentation do I need for fire truck financing?

Municipal: procurement docs, council approval. Commercial: business tax returns, financials, equipment specs. Volunteer: 501(c)(3) and department financials.

What affects fire truck pricing?

Type (pumper, aerial, ladder, rescue), pump capacity, tank size, aerial reach, and manufacturer.
