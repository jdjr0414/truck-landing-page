How Much Does a Fire Truck Cost?

Fire truck costs vary by type (pumper, aerial, ladder, rescue). New pumpers range from $300,000 to $600,000. Aerials and ladder trucks can exceed $1M. Used fire apparatus ranges from $100,000 to $500,000+. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new fire truck cost?

New fire trucks range from $300,000 to $1M+ depending on type. Pumpers: $300,000–$600,000. Aerials and ladder trucks: $800,000–$1.5M+.

How much does a used fire truck cost?

Used fire trucks range from $100,000 to $500,000+ depending on type, age, and condition.

What affects fire truck pricing?

Type (pumper, aerial, ladder, rescue), pump capacity, tank size, aerial reach, and manufacturer.

Can I finance a fire truck?

Yes. Fire truck financing is available for municipal and industrial fire departments. Terms may extend 10–15 years.
