Common Questions

How much does a new bucket truck cost?

New bucket trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on aerial reach (35–65+ feet) and chassis.

How much does a used bucket truck cost?

Used bucket trucks range from $60,000 to $200,000 depending on age, aerial reach, and condition.

What credit score do I need for bucket truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Contract revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment for a bucket truck?

Typically 10–30%. New units often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and utility contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup utility contractors finance bucket trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer contractors. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts. Industry experience helps.

How long are bucket truck loan terms?

New bucket trucks: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and aerial condition. Aerial equipment may qualify for longer terms.

How fast can bucket truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used bucket truck?

Yes. Used bucket truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Lenders consider aerial condition and certifications.

What documentation do I need for bucket truck financing?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of contracts, and equipment details. Aerial certifications may be required.

What affects bucket truck pricing?

Aerial working height, platform capacity, chassis make/model, and condition. Over-center aerials cost more than rear-mount.
