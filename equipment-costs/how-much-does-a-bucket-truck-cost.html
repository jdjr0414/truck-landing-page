How Much Does a Bucket Truck Cost?

Bucket truck costs vary by aerial reach, chassis, and condition. New bucket trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+. Used units range from $60,000 to $200,000. Working height and aerial type significantly affect price. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new bucket truck cost?

New bucket trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on aerial reach (35–65+ feet) and chassis.

How much does a used bucket truck cost?

Used bucket trucks range from $60,000 to $200,000 depending on age, aerial reach, and condition.

What affects bucket truck pricing?

Aerial working height, platform capacity, chassis make/model, and condition. Over-center aerials cost more than rear-mount.

Can I finance a bucket truck?

Yes. Bucket truck financing is widely available. Down payments typically run 10–30%.
