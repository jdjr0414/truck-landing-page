How Much Does a Boom Truck Cost?

A new knuckle boom truck (5-ton capacity) costs $80,000–$150,000. A new straight boom crane truck (20–30 ton) costs $150,000–$350,000. Used boom trucks range from $35,000 to $200,000 depending on capacity, condition, and boom type. This guide covers pricing by capacity, knuckle vs. straight boom, and financing options.

Common Uses for Boom Trucks

Logging and tree service: knuckle boom trucks are the standard loading tool in timber harvesting. Forestry-configured knuckle booms load logs at the landing and are the most common use of this equipment type in rural markets.

Construction material placement: placing structural steel, precast panels, roofing materials, and mechanical equipment in commercial construction. Medium knuckle boom trucks are often more cost-effective than renting a mobile crane for routine lifts.

Utility and telecom: pole setting, transformer installation, and cable reel handling. Utility companies and contractors use boom trucks for roadside and overhead work.

Scrap and recycling: scrap yards use knuckle boom trucks to load and sort ferrous and non-ferrous material. The grapple attachment replaces the hook for bulk material handling.

Certification Requirements

OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1427 requires crane operators on construction sites to be certified by an accredited certification program for cranes over 2,000 lbs capacity. The NCCCO (National Commission for the Certification of Crane Operators) certification is the most widely recognized and accepted standard.

Knuckle boom operators on logging operations may be subject to different requirements under forestry safety standards. Operators should verify applicable OSHA standards and state regulations for their specific application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a boom truck require a CDL?

Yes. Most commercial boom trucks exceed 26,001 lbs GVWR and require a Class B CDL (single-unit vehicle over 26,001 lbs) or Class A CDL (combination vehicle). Operators should verify their specific truck's GVWR and applicable state CDL thresholds. See commercial truck license requirements.

How do I finance a used boom truck?

Used boom trucks are financed through commercial equipment lenders and truck specialty lenders. The truck and boom typically serve as collateral. Boom condition — hydraulic system, boom wear, cylinder integrity — is evaluated. See boom truck financing for more detail.
