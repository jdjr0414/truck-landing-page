How Much Does a Semi Trailer Cost?

Semi trailer prices vary significantly by type. A new 53-ft dry van costs $35,000–$55,000. A new flatbed costs $35,000–$55,000. A new reefer trailer costs $60,000–$100,000. Used trailers range from $10,000 for older dry vans to $65,000 for late-model refrigerated trailers. This guide covers pricing by trailer type, 53-ft vs 48-ft, and financing options.

Key Takeaways

  • New 53-ft dry van trailer: $35,000–$55,000
  • New 53-ft reefer trailer: $60,000–$100,000
  • Used dry van: $10,000–$40,000
  • Trailers can be financed separately from tractors

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a CDL to own a semi trailer?

You need a Class A CDL to drive a tractor-trailer combination over 26,001 lbs GVWR with a towed trailer over 10,000 lbs. Owning the trailer itself does not require a CDL — only operating it on a public road does. See commercial truck license requirements.

What is the most common semi trailer for owner-operators?

The 53-ft dry van is the most common trailer for new owner-operators due to its widespread freight availability, intermodal compatibility, and strong resale market. Flatbed trailers are popular for specialized freight at higher per-mile rates but require special loading equipment and tarping skills. For the tractor, see how much does a semi truck cost.
