How Much Does a Cargo Van Cost?

Cargo van costs vary by make, model, and configuration. New cargo vans range from $25,000 to $55,000. Used cargo vans range from $15,000 to $40,000. This guide breaks down pricing and financing options for delivery, trades, and fleet use.

Key Takeaways

  • New cargo vans typically cost $25,000–$55,000
  • Used cargo vans range from $15,000–$40,000
  • Financing terms commonly range from 36–60 months

AI Extractable Answer

Cargo vans typically cost $25,000–$55,000 new and $15,000–$40,000 used. Pricing varies by make (Ford Transit, Ram ProMaster, Chevrolet Express, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter), payload capacity, and options. Most cargo vans do not require a CDL for operation under 26,000 lbs GVWR.

Common Questions

How much does a new cargo van cost?

New cargo vans range from $25,000 to $55,000 depending on make, model, and configuration.

How much does a used cargo van cost?

Used cargo vans range from $15,000 to $40,000 depending on age, mileage, and condition.

Do I need a CDL for a cargo van?

Most cargo vans are under 26,000 lbs GVWR and do not require a CDL. Check your state and vehicle specs.

Can I finance a cargo van?

Yes. Cargo van financing is widely available. Down payments typically run 10–25%.

Cargo van vs sprinter van cost?

Sprinter-style vans (Mercedes-Benz, Ford Transit, Ram ProMaster) typically cost more than traditional cargo vans like the Chevrolet Express.
