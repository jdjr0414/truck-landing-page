How Much Does a Water Truck Cost?

A new construction water truck costs $80,000–$130,000 for a Class 6 unit with a 2,000–3,000 gallon tank. Class 7–8 water trucks with 4,000–5,000 gallon tanks run $110,000–$180,000. Used water trucks range from $35,000 for older units to $100,000+ for late-model trucks in good condition.

Key Takeaways

  • New 2,000–3,000 gallon water truck (Class 6): $80,000 – $130,000
  • New 4,000–5,000 gallon water truck (Class 7–8): $110,000 – $180,000
  • Used water trucks: $35,000 – $100,000
  • Class B CDL typically required when loaded

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to buy or rent a water truck?

Renting makes sense for short-duration projects (1–4 weeks) or infrequent needs. Rental rates for construction water trucks run $800–$2,000 per week depending on tank size and region. For contractors who need a water truck regularly across multiple projects, ownership becomes more economical than renting after approximately 6–12 months of typical use. Water trucks also have strong residual value, making them reasonable purchases even for smaller contractors.

What is the difference between a water truck and a tanker truck?

A water truck is specifically set up for distributing water on construction sites — it has spray bars, a front-mounted cannon, or gravity-feed systems for dispensing water at a controlled rate across a wide area. A tanker truck transports liquid cargo (including water, but also fuel, chemicals, or food liquids) in bulk without a spray distribution system. See our tanker truck cost guide for tanker pricing.
