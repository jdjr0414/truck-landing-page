Overview

An asphalt paving business installs and maintains asphalt surfaces–driveways, parking lots, roads, and commercial sites. Full paving requires a dump truck to haul hot mix, a paver to lay asphalt, a roller to compact, and a tack truck for emulsion. Sealcoating is a lower-barrier entry with spray systems and squeegees. Revenue comes from per-square-foot or per-ton pricing. Success depends on equipment, skilled labor, CDL compliance, and relationships with general contractors and municipalities.

Customers and Revenue

Primary customers: homeowners, property managers, commercial property owners, general contractors, and municipal governments. Revenue from driveway paving ($2,000–$15,000+), parking lot projects ($10,000–$500,000+), municipal road contracts, sealcoating ($0.15–$0.35/sq ft), and crack sealing. Municipal work requires surety bonding. Building relationships with contractors and property managers drives referrals.

Equipment

Core equipment: dump truck for hauling hot mix, asphalt paver, roller (compactor), and tack truck. See dump truck financing and construction truck financing. Dump truck: $80,000–$180,000 new. Paver: $50,000–$250,000+. Roller: $25,000–$80,000. Tack truck: $30,000–$80,000. Small operations may subcontract hauling or rent equipment initially.

Typical Equipment Needed

  • Dump truck (hot mix hauling)
  • Asphalt paver
  • Roller / compactor
  • Tack truck (emulsion)
  • Milling machine (optional, for larger operations)
  • Sealcoating equipment (if offering sealcoating)

Licensing and Regulatory Requirements

Asphalt paving requires CDL and construction-related permits. See commercial truck license requirements.

CDL: Required for dump trucks over 26,000 lbs GVWR. Class A for tractor-trailer combinations; Class B for single heavy vehicles. Paver and roller operators may need CDL if equipment exceeds weight thresholds.

DOT: USDOT number if interstate. Many paving operations are intrastate.

State and local: Contractor licensing in some states. Business registration. Municipal work may require prequalification and bonding.

Environmental: Asphalt plants and storage may require permits. Hot mix handling has environmental considerations.

Disclaimer: Licensing and permit requirements vary by state and locality. Verify with your state licensing board and DOT before operating.

Typical License Requirements

  • Class A or Class B CDL (for dump trucks)
  • Contractor license (state-dependent)
  • USDOT number (if interstate)
  • State business registration

Insurance

Commercial auto liability, general liability, and workers comp. Municipal and commercial contracts often require surety bonds. Equipment coverage for pavers and rollers.

Typical Insurance Needs

  • Commercial auto liability
  • General liability
  • Workers comp
  • Surety bonds (for municipal work)

Financing

Dump truck financing and construction truck financing are available from commercial lenders. Down payment varies by credit–strong credit may qualify for low or no down payment; new businesses often need 20–30%. See commercial vehicle financing for options. Equipment lenders finance pavers, rollers, and tack trucks separately or as packages.

Common Mistakes

Undercapitalization; paving is equipment-intensive and material costs are high. Operating without CDL–dump truck drivers must be properly licensed. Skipping bonding for municipal work. Underestimating seasonal demand (paving is weather-dependent). Failing to maintain equipment–downtime costs jobs. Taking on projects beyond capacity.

Common Questions

How much does it cost to start an asphalt paving business?

Startup costs typically range from $150,000 to $500,000+ including dump truck, paver, roller, tack truck, insurance, and working capital.

Do I need a CDL for an asphalt paving business?

Yes for dump trucks. Dump trucks over 26,000 lbs GVWR require Class A or Class B CDL.

What equipment does an asphalt paving business need?

Dump truck, asphalt paver, roller, tack truck, and sometimes a milling machine. Small operations may subcontract hauling.

Can I finance asphalt paving equipment as a new business?

Yes. Down payment varies by credit–new businesses often need 20–30%. Dump truck and construction equipment financing is available.

Is a down payment always required for dump truck financing?

No. Down payment varies by credit. Strong credit may qualify for 0% down. New businesses often need 20–30%.

Can I start with just sealcoating?

Yes. Sealcoating requires less equipment–spray systems, squeegees, and a truck. You can add full paving later.

What are the main revenue sources for asphalt paving?

Driveways, parking lots, municipal road contracts, sealcoating, and crack sealing. Municipal work requires bonding.

How much down payment for a dump truck as a new paving business?

Typically 20–30%. Down payment varies by credit–strong credit may qualify for 0% down.
