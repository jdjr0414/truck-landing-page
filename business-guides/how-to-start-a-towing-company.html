How to Start a Towing Company

Starting a towing company requires equipment, insurance, permits, and contracts. This guide walks through the steps: forming your business, obtaining permits, purchasing or financing a tow truck, securing insurance, and landing AAA or insurance network contracts. Revenue comes from per-call fees, police rotation, and direct customers.

Quick Answer

To start a towing company: form an LLC, obtain local permits, purchase or finance a tow truck ($60,000–$130,000 new), get insurance, and apply for AAA or insurance network contracts. Startup costs: $50,000–$150,000. See tow truck cost and tow truck financing.

Step 1: Form Your Business

Register as an LLC. Obtain an EIN. Open a business bank account. Check local towing and impound permit requirements.

Step 2: Choose Your Tow Truck Type

Flatbed (versatile), wheel-lift (lower cost), or integrated (heavy recovery). See tow truck cost guide. Flatbeds are common for AAA and insurance work.

Step 3: Purchase or Finance a Tow Truck

Tow truck financing typically requires 20–30% down for new businesses. Proof of AAA or insurance contracts strengthens applications.

Step 4: Insurance

Commercial auto liability, cargo insurance, and garage keepers. AAA and insurance networks often require $1M+ liability. Shop specialty towing insurers.

Step 5: Land Contracts

Apply for AAA contractor program, insurance network (GEICO, State Farm, etc.), and police rotation. Each has different requirements and approval processes.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need to finance a tow truck as a new business?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments (25–30%). AAA contracts help.

How much down payment for a tow truck as a new business?

Typically 20–30%. New towing businesses often need more. Proof of AAA or insurance contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup towing companies get financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with new towing businesses. Expect 20–30% down. Proof of contracts strengthens applications.

How long are tow truck loan terms for new businesses?

New tow trucks: 36–60 months. Used: 24–48 months. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can new towing companies get financing approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Having contracts ready speeds the process.

Can I finance a used tow truck as a new business?

Yes. Used tow trucks ($30,000–$90,000) reduce startup cost vs new ($60,000–$150,000). Expect 20–30% down.

What documentation do new towing companies need?

Business formation docs, tax returns (if any), bank statements, proof of contracts or revenue, and equipment details. CDL may be required.

How much does it cost to start a towing company?

Startup costs typically range from $50,000 to $150,000 including tow truck, insurance, permits, and operating capital.

What type of tow truck should I start with?

Flatbed tow trucks are versatile for light and medium duty. Wheel-lift is lower cost. Choose based on your target market.

How do I get AAA or insurance contracts?

Apply through AAA's contractor program or insurance network. Requirements include insurance limits, response time, and geographic coverage.
