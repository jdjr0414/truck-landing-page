How to Start a Hydro Excavation Business

Starting a hydro excavation business requires a hydrovac truck, operator training, and utility or contractor contracts. This guide walks through the steps: forming your business, obtaining training, purchasing or financing a hydrovac, securing insurance, and landing utility or contractor work. Revenue comes from daylighting, potholing, and trenching.

Quick Answer

To start a hydro excavation business: form an LLC, obtain operator training, purchase or finance a hydrovac truck ($200,000–$450,000 new), get insurance, and secure utility or contractor contracts. Startup costs: $150,000–$400,000+. See hydro excavation truck financing.

Step 1: Form Your Business

Register as an LLC. Obtain an EIN. Open a business bank account. Research local utility and contractor requirements.

Step 2: Operator Training

Hydrovac operator training, confined space, and OSHA certifications. Some utilities require pre-qualification or approved vendor status.

Step 3: Purchase or Finance a Hydrovac Truck

Hydro excavation truck financing is available. New trucks: $200,000–$450,000. Tank capacity and vacuum power affect price. Used: $80,000–$200,000.

Step 4: Insurance

Commercial auto liability, general liability, and workers comp. Utilities often require $2M+ liability and specific endorsements.

Step 5: Land Contracts

Contact gas, electric, and telecom utilities; municipalities; and excavation contractors. Daylighting and potholing are core services. Consider subcontracting initially.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need to finance a hydrovac truck as a new business?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments (25–30%). Utility contracts help.

How much down payment for a hydrovac truck as a new business?

Typically 20–30%. New hydro excavation businesses often need more. Proof of utility or contractor contracts can reduce requirements.

Can startup hydro excavation businesses get financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with new hydro excavation businesses. Expect 20–30% down. Utility or contractor contracts strengthen applications.

How long are hydrovac truck loan terms for new businesses?

New hydrovac: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can new hydro excavation businesses get financing approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Having utility contracts ready speeds the process.

Can I finance a used hydrovac truck as a new business?

Yes. Used hydrovac trucks can reduce startup cost. Expect 20–30% down. Lower purchase price means smaller loan amount.

What documentation do new hydro excavation businesses need?

Business formation docs, tax returns (if any), bank statements, proof of utility/contractor contracts, and equipment details.

How much does it cost to start a hydro excavation business?

Startup costs typically range from $150,000 to $400,000+ including hydrovac truck, insurance, and operating capital.

Who hires hydro excavation companies?

Utilities (gas, electric, telecom), municipalities, contractors, and pipeline companies. Daylighting and potholing are common services.

What certifications do I need for hydro excavation?

Operator training, confined space, and possibly OSHA 10/30. Some utilities require specific certifications.
