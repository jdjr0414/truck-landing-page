Common Questions

What credit score do environmental services need?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Contract revenue can offset weaker credit.

What down payment for environmental vehicles?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established revenue can reduce requirements.

Can startup environmental companies get financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer businesses. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue. Industry experience helps.

How long are environmental vehicle loan terms?

New vac trucks: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and condition. Hydro excavation units may qualify for longer terms.

How fast can environmental vehicle financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used vac truck?

Yes. Used vac truck and environmental vehicle financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do environmental services need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of contracts or revenue, and equipment details. Disposal permits may be required.

What do environmental vehicles cost?

Vac trucks: $150,000–$350,000. Hydro excavation: $200,000–$400,000+. Septic vac trucks: $150,000–$300,000. Costs vary by configuration.

What vehicles do environmental services use?

Vac trucks, hydro excavation trucks, septic vac trucks, and industrial vacuum trucks.
