Frequently Asked Questions

What trucks do waste management companies finance?

Garbage trucks, refuse trucks, recycling trucks, and transfer vehicles. Waste management includes residential, commercial, and municipal hauling.

How much does a garbage truck cost to finance?

Garbage trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on type. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can municipalities finance garbage trucks?

Yes. Municipalities often use lease-purchase or municipal financing for refuse trucks.
