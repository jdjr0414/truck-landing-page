Municipal Vehicle Financing

Financing for Municipal Vehicles & Public Fleet Equipment

Municipal vehicle financing covers fire trucks, garbage trucks, street sweepers, snow plows, and support vehicles for cities, counties, and public agencies. Municipal entities often use lease-purchase or municipal financing structures. Terms may extend 10–15 years for fire apparatus. Frequently Asked Questions

What is municipal vehicle financing?

Municipal vehicle financing covers equipment for cities, counties, and public agencies. Often uses lease-purchase or municipal structures.

What vehicles do municipalities finance?

Fire trucks, garbage trucks, street sweepers, snow plows, and support vehicles.

What terms are available for municipal vehicles?

Terms may extend 10–15 years for fire trucks and specialty apparatus. Garbage trucks and sweepers often 60–84 months.
