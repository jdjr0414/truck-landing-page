Frequently Asked Questions

What trucks do oilfield companies finance?

Vac trucks, tankers, water trucks, and support vehicles. Oilfield truck financing covers equipment for well servicing and energy support.

Is oilfield truck financing available in downturns?

Lenders may tighten during energy downturns. Strong revenue and contracts support approval.

What down payment for oilfield trucks?

Typically 10–30%. Cyclical revenue may require higher down payments.
