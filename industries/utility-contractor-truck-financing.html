Frequently Asked Questions

What trucks do utility contractors finance?

Bucket trucks, vac trucks, service trucks, and support vehicles. Utility contractors need aerial and excavation equipment for line work.

How much does a bucket truck cost to finance?

Bucket trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+ depending on aerial reach. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used bucket truck?

Yes. Used bucket truck and utility truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.
