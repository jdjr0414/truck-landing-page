Common Questions

What credit score do farms need for truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Farm revenue and land value can offset weaker credit.

What down payment for farm trucks?

Typically 10–30%. Seasonal revenue may affect requirements. Strong credit and established operations can reduce requirements.

Can new farms get agriculture truck financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer operations. Expect 20–30% down and proof of revenue or land. Established farms have easier access.

How long are agriculture truck loan terms?

New trucks: 48–72 months. Used: 24–60 months depending on age and type. Grain haulers and dump trucks may qualify for longer terms.

How fast can agriculture truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Seasonal timing may affect lender availability. Pre-approval speeds the process.

Can I finance a used grain truck?

Yes. Used agriculture truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Advance rates may be lower than for new.

What documentation do farms need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, proof of farm revenue, and equipment details. Schedule F may be used for farm income. Seasonal revenue is considered.

What do farm trucks cost?

Grain haulers: $80,000–$180,000. Dump trucks: $80,000–$180,000. Farm service trucks: $60,000–$150,000. Costs vary by configuration.

What trucks do farms finance?

Grain haulers, dump trucks, flatbeds, and farm service trucks. Agriculture equipment financing covers trucks used in farming operations.
