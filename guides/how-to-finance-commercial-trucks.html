How to Finance Commercial Trucks: Step-by-Step

This guide walks through the process of financing commercial trucks—from gathering documentation to closing. Whether you're financing a semi truck, dump truck, or bucket truck, the steps are similar. See truck financing guide and how commercial truck loans work for more detail.

Quick Answer

To finance commercial trucks, gather business documentation (tax returns, P&L, bank statements), equipment details, and apply through a lender or broker. Most programs require 10–30% down; approval typically takes 24–72 hours for qualified borrowers.

Step 1: Gather Documentation

Collect business tax returns, profit and loss statements, bank statements, driver's license, and equipment details (invoice, spec sheet, or listing). Some programs require fewer documents for smaller amounts.

Step 2: Identify Equipment

Know the make, model, year, VIN, and price. For used equipment, document mileage and condition. Lenders use this to determine advance rate and terms.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need to finance commercial trucks?

Many programs require 650+ for competitive terms. Some lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Business revenue can offset weaker credit.

How much down payment is required?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with $0 down.

Can startup businesses finance commercial trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue. Time in business under 12 months may limit options.

How long are commercial truck loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage. Terms match equipment useful life.

How fast can I get commercial truck financing approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval can speed the process once equipment is selected.

Can I finance a used commercial truck?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically shorter (24–60 months) than for new equipment. Advance rates may be lower.

What documentation do I need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details (invoice, spec sheet, or listing). Some programs require fewer documents for smaller amounts.

What do commercial trucks typically cost?

Semi trucks: $120,000–$200,000. Dump trucks: $80,000–$180,000. Bucket trucks: $90,000–$250,000. Vac trucks: $150,000–$350,000.

What is the first step to finance a commercial truck?

Gather business documentation and equipment details. Compare programs from multiple lenders. Pre-approval can speed the process.
