Common Questions

What credit score do fleets need for financing?

Many fleet programs prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Established fleets with strong revenue may qualify for better rates. Volume can improve terms.

How much down payment do fleet programs require?

Typically 10–30% per unit. Fleet programs may offer flexibility. Strong credit and established revenue can reduce requirements.

Can startup fleets get financing?

Less common. Fleet programs typically require 2+ units and established operations. New fleets may need to start with single-unit financing.

How long are fleet loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months. Master agreements may allow consistent terms across replacement cycles.

How fast can fleet financing be approved?

Streamlined documentation can speed approval. Simple fleet additions: 1–5 business days. New fleet relationships: 1–2 weeks.

Can fleets finance used trucks?

Yes. Fleet replacement programs often include used equipment. Terms are shorter than for new. Volume pricing may apply.

What documentation do fleets need?

Business tax returns, fleet financials, bank statements, and equipment details. Master agreements may reduce documentation for repeat purchases.

What does fleet financing cost?

Fleet programs may offer volume pricing and competitive rates. Compare fleet vs single-unit programs. Dedicated relationship managers may be available.

How many trucks qualify as a fleet?

Typically 2+ units. Some programs start at 3–5 units. Fleet programs vary by lender.
