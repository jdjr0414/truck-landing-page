Commercial Fleet Financing Guide

Quick Answer

Fleet financing covers multiple trucks (typically 2+ units) and may offer volume pricing, streamlined documentation, and master agreements. Fleet programs can provide better rates and dedicated relationship managers compared to single-unit purchases.

Fleet vs Single-Unit Financing

Fleet programs may offer volume pricing, streamlined documentation, and master agreements. Single-unit purchases follow standard equipment financing. Fleet programs typically require 2+ units; some start at 3–5.

Fleet Program Benefits

  • Volume pricing on rate or fees
  • Streamlined documentation for repeat purchases
  • Master agreements for ongoing fleet growth
  • Dedicated fleet relationship managers

Fleet Replacement Cycles

Fleets typically replace trucks on 5–7 year cycles (semi trucks) or 4–6 years (dump trucks, vocational equipment). Master agreements let you add or replace units without full re-approval each time. Plan financing to align with replacement schedule; refinancing or trade-in can free equity for new purchases. See Equipment Lifespan and Truck Loan Refinancing.

Fleet Documentation Requirements

Initial fleet application: business tax returns (2 years), fleet financials, bank statements, and equipment details for each unit. Master agreements may reduce documentation for subsequent purchases–lenders use existing relationship and payment history. Large fleets may provide fleet schedules and depreciation schedules.

When Fleet Financing Makes Sense

Fleet programs suit companies adding 2+ units, planning replacement cycles, or growing consistently. Single-unit purchases use standard equipment financing. If you plan to add trucks within 12–24 months, ask about fleet programs when applying for the first unit; some lenders grandfather you into fleet pricing.

Common Questions

What credit score do fleets need for financing?

Many fleet programs prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Established fleets with strong revenue may qualify for better rates. Volume can improve terms.

How much down payment do fleet programs require?

Typically 10–30% per unit. Fleet programs may offer flexibility. Strong credit and established revenue can reduce requirements.

Can startup fleets get financing?

Less common. Fleet programs typically require 2+ units and established operations. New fleets may need to start with single-unit financing.

How long are fleet loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months. Master agreements may allow consistent terms across replacement cycles.

How fast can fleet financing be approved?

Streamlined documentation can speed approval. Simple fleet additions: 1–5 business days. New fleet relationships: 1–2 weeks.

Can fleets finance used trucks?

Yes. Fleet replacement programs often include used equipment. Terms are shorter than for new. Volume pricing may apply.

What documentation do fleets need?

Business tax returns, fleet financials, bank statements, and equipment details. Master agreements may reduce documentation for repeat purchases.

What does fleet financing cost?

Fleet programs may offer volume pricing and competitive rates. Compare fleet vs single-unit programs. Dedicated relationship managers may be available.

What is fleet financing?

How many trucks qualify as a fleet?

Typically 2+ units. Some programs start at 3–5 units. Fleet programs vary by lender.
