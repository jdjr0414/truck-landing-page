Commercial Vehicle Registration by State

Commercial vehicle registration varies by state. IRP (International Registration Plan) and IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement) apply to interstate operations. This guide covers what each state typically requires and how to register. See DOT registration requirements for federal rules.

Key Takeaways

  • IRP: apportioned registration for 26,001+ lb vehicles
  • IFTA: apportioned fuel tax for interstate
  • Base state: where vehicle is garaged
  • State DMV and FMCSA are authoritative sources

Definition

Commercial vehicle registration by state refers to the process of registering trucks and trailers for interstate or intrastate operation. IRP (International Registration Plan) apportions registration across jurisdictions. IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement) apportions fuel taxes. Requirements depend on vehicle weight, use, and base state. See DOT registration requirements.

IRP (International Registration Plan)

IRP apportions registration fees across states where you operate. Required for vehicles 26,001+ lbs GVWR or used in interstate commerce. You register in your base state (where the vehicle is garaged) and receive apportioned plates. All 48 contiguous states plus D.C. and Canadian provinces participate.

IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement)

IFTA apportions fuel tax across jurisdictions. Required for vehicles 26,001+ lbs or used in interstate commerce. File quarterly returns through your base state. See what is IFTA.

State-Specific Requirements

Each state has weight fees, use taxes, and local requirements. Intrastate-only operations may have different rules. Hotshot and box truck operators under 26,001 lbs may not need IRP/IFTA in some cases. Verify with your state DMV and FMCSA.

How to Register

Base state: where your vehicle is primarily garaged. Apply for IRP and IFTA through your base state DMV. USDOT number through FMCSA. See DOT registration requirements and DOT number for trucking business.

Factors That Affect Registration

Vehicle weight: Heavier vehicles typically pay more in IRP and weight fees. Miles per state: IRP apportions fees by miles traveled in each jurisdiction. Base state: Where you garage the vehicle determines which DMV processes IRP/IFTA. Intrastate vs interstate: Intrastate-only may have different rules. Hotshot and box truck under 26,001 lbs may not need IRP/IFTA in some cases. See owner operator business checklist.

Common Questions

What is IRP registration?

International Registration Plan (IRP) apportions registration fees across states where you operate. Required for vehicles 26,001+ lbs or used in interstate commerce.

What is IFTA?

International Fuel Tax Agreement. Apportions fuel tax across states. Required for vehicles 26,001+ lbs or used in interstate commerce.

Do I register in my home state?

Yes. Base state is typically where the vehicle is garaged. IRP and IFTA are filed through your base state.

Do hotshot trucks need IRP and IFTA?

If GCWR is 26,001+ lbs and you operate interstate, yes. Under 26,001 lbs may not need IRP/IFTA in some cases. Verify with your state.

How much does IRP cost?

Typically $500–$2,000+ annually depending on weight and miles per state. Varies by base state.

When are IFTA returns due?

Quarterly. Due by the last day of the month following each quarter (e.g., Q1 due April 30). See what is IFTA.
