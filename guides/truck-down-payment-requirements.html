Truck Down Payment Requirements

Down payments for commercial truck financing typically range from 10% to 30%. New trucks from dealers often allow 10–15% for qualified borrowers. Used trucks may require 20–30%. Owner-operators and new entrants often need 20–30%. See new vs used truck financing, semi truck financing, and truck financing guide.

Down Payment by Equipment Type

  • New trucks: 10–15% for qualified borrowers
  • Used trucks: 20–30%
  • Owner-operators: 20–30%
  • Startups: 20–30%

Factors That Affect Down Payment

Credit score, time in business, revenue, equipment age, and equipment type. Strong credit and revenue can reduce requirements. Poor credit or older equipment may increase requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much down payment for a new truck?

New trucks often allow 10–15% down for qualified borrowers. Strong credit and revenue can reduce requirements.

How much down payment for a used truck?

Used trucks typically require 20–30% down. Older or higher-mileage units may require more.

Can I get truck financing with no down payment?

Zero down is rare for commercial trucks. Some programs offer 5–10% for highly qualified borrowers. Most require 10–30%.
