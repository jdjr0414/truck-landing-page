Truck Down Payment Requirements

Down payments for commercial truck financing typically range from 10% to 30%. New trucks from dealers often allow 10–15% for qualified borrowers. Used trucks may require 20–30%. Owner-operators and new entrants often need 20–30%. See new vs used truck financing, semi truck financing, and truck financing guide.

Common Questions

What credit score affects down payment requirements?

Strong credit (720+) often qualifies for $0 down. 650–719 may need 10–15%. Below 650 may require 20–30%. Down payments are risk-based.

How much down payment for a new truck?

New trucks often allow 10–15% down for qualified borrowers. Strong credit and revenue can reduce or eliminate requirements.

How much down payment for a used truck?

Used trucks typically require 20–30% down. Older or higher-mileage units may require more. Advance rates cap at 70–80% for used.

Can startup trucking companies get financing with less down?

Startups typically need 20–30% down. Proof of contracts or carrier agreements can help. Some lenders specialize in first-time buyers.

How long are truck loan terms with different down payments?

Terms are tied to equipment, not down payment. New: 60–84 months. Used: 24–60 months. Larger down payments may improve rate, not necessarily term.

How fast can I get approved with a low down payment?

Approval speed depends on application completeness, not down payment. Simple applications: 1–3 days. Strong credit with $0 down: same timeline.

Can I finance a used truck with a smaller down payment?

Used trucks typically require 20–30% down. Strong credit and established revenue may allow less. Advance rates are lower for used equipment.

What documents affect down payment requirements?

Business tax returns, bank statements, and P&L show revenue. Strong revenue and credit can reduce down payment. Lenders evaluate the full profile.

Can businesses qualify with no down payment?

Yes. Strong credit and established businesses often qualify with $0 down. Down payments are risk-based, not mandatory for all borrowers.
