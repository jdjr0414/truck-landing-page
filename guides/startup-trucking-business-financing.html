Startup Trucking Business Financing

Startup trucking businesses face stricter underwriting. Lenders prefer 12–24 months in business, but some programs work with new carriers. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts or carrier agreements, and strong personal credit. See owner-operator truck financing, semi truck financing, and trucking company financing.

Quick Answer

Startup trucking companies can get financing with 20–30% down, proof of contracts or carrier agreements, and strong personal credit (650+). Some lenders specialize in first-time truck buyers; letters of intent from carriers strengthen applications.

What Lenders Look For

  • Business plan and revenue projections

Common Questions

What credit score do startup trucking companies need?

650+ for competitive terms. Some lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Strong personal credit offsets lack of business history.

How much down payment do startup trucking companies need?

Typically 20–30%. First-time truck buyers often need more. Proof of contracts or carrier agreements can help reduce requirements.

Can startup trucking companies get financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts or load history, and strong personal credit.

How long are startup truck loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months. Startups may face shorter terms until they establish track record.

How fast can startup trucking companies get approved?

1–3 business days for simple applications. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval speeds the process once equipment is selected.

Can startups finance used trucks?

Yes. Used truck financing is available for startups. Lower purchase price reduces loan amount. Expect 20–30% down and shorter terms.

What documentation do startup trucking companies need?

Business plan, proof of contracts or carrier agreements, personal credit 650+, bank statements, and equipment details. Letters of intent from carriers help.

What do trucks cost for new trucking companies?

New semi: $120,000–$200,000. Used semi: $40,000–$120,000. Startups often buy used to reduce upfront cost and monthly payments.

Is it harder to finance a truck as a startup?

Yes. Startups face stricter underwriting. Down payments are higher; some lenders specialize in first-time truck buyers.
