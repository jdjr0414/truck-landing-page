What Is a Load Board?

Load boards are online marketplaces where shippers and brokers post freight and carriers search for loads. Carriers pay a subscription fee to access loads. Hotshot operators use flatbed and gooseneck filters to find compatible freight. Load volume and quality vary by board, region, and season. Some boards specialize in certain freight types (e.g., oilfield, expedited). Competition for loads can be high; quick response improves booking success.

What to Look For

Filter by trailer type (flatbed, gooseneck), weight, and lane. Compare subscription fees. Some offer free trials. Load volume varies by region and season. Oilfield regions like Texas and the Dakotas often have more hotshot freight. Construction and agriculture lanes peak in spring and fall. Check each board's flatbed and expedited load counts before subscribing. Some boards offer mobile apps for on-the-go load searching.

When to Use Load Boards

Load boards work well for spot market freight, testing new lanes, and filling gaps between dedicated contracts. They're less ideal as your only source—combine with broker and direct shipper relationships for stability. New operators often start with load boards while building their network. Alternatives to Load Boards

Direct shippers (manufacturers, dealers), freight brokers, dispatch services. Building relationships often yields steadier lanes and better rates than spot market load boards. Direct shippers eliminate the broker margin. Freight brokers post loads on boards but also work with carriers directly—getting on a broker's preferred carrier list can mean first access to loads. Dispatch services charge a fee or percentage but handle load finding for you. See how to get hotshot loads for a full strategy breakdown.

Tips for Using Load Boards

  • Complete your profile fully—MC, insurance, trailer types.
  • Set up lane alerts for your preferred routes.
  • Respond quickly—loads are often booked within minutes.
  • Compare free trials before committing to a subscription.
  • Respond quickly to load posts—competition is high.

Common Questions

What are the best load boards for hotshot?

DAT, Truckstop.com, 123Loadboard, and specialized hotshot boards. Compare fees and load volume.

How much do load boards cost?

$50–$150/month typical. Some offer free trials or pay-per-load options.

Can I get hotshot loads without a load board?

Yes. Direct shippers, brokers, and dispatch services. Load boards are one option.

Do load boards have free trials?

Some do. Check each provider for trial offers before committing.

Which load board has the most flatbed loads?

DAT and Truckstop.com typically have high flatbed volume. Regional boards may vary.

Can I use multiple load boards?

Yes. Many operators use 2–3 boards to maximize load options. Compare fees—some offer bundles or discounts for multiple services.

How do I get loads as a new carrier on load boards?

Complete your profile with MC, insurance, and trailer types. Set up alerts for your preferred lanes. Respond quickly to posts—many loads are booked within minutes.

What trailer types should I filter for hotshot?

Flatbed, gooseneck, flatbed with gooseneck. Some boards use "hotshot" or "expedited" as filters. Match your actual equipment. Incorrect filters may show loads you cannot haul.

Are load boards worth it for new hotshot operators?

Often yes. Load boards provide quick access to loads while you build broker and direct shipper relationships. Use free trials to test before committing.
