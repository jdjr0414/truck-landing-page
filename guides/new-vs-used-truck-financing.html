New vs Used Truck Financing: Compare Your Options

New truck financing typically offers longer terms (60–84 months), lower rates, and lower down payments (10–15%). Used truck financing is more restrictive: shorter terms (24–60 months), higher rates, and higher down payments (20–30%). Lenders focus on remaining useful life for used equipment. See semi truck financing and truck financing guide for vehicle-specific details.

Key Takeaways

  • Financing terms commonly range from 24-60 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

When to Choose New vs Used

New trucks make sense when you want maximum term, lowest payment, and warranty. Used trucks make sense when upfront cost matters more and you're comfortable with shorter terms. Compare total cost of ownership.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need for new vs used truck financing?

Same credit requirements apply. New trucks may qualify for better rates and terms. Used trucks with lower loan amounts may be easier for marginal credit.

How much down payment for new vs used trucks?

New: 10–15% for qualified borrowers. Used: 20–30% typical. Strong credit can reduce requirements for both.

Can startups finance new or used trucks?

Yes. Startups often buy used to reduce cost. Expect 20–30% down for either. Used may have lower monthly payments due to lower purchase price.

How long are loan terms for new vs used trucks?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage. Terms match remaining useful life.

How fast is approval for new vs used truck financing?

Same timeline: 1–5 business days for simple applications. Dealer purchases may have streamlined approval. Used private party may take longer.

Can I finance used commercial trucks?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available. Terms are shorter (24–60 months). Advance rates cap at 70–80% vs 80–90% for new.

What documentation do I need for new vs used?

Same: business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, equipment details. Used may require inspection or appraisal.

What is the cost difference between new and used trucks?

New semi: $120,000–$200,000. Used semi: $40,000–$120,000. Used typically costs 40–60% less but has shorter financing terms.

Is it easier to finance new or used trucks?

New trucks typically qualify for better terms—longer terms, lower rates, lower down payments. Used trucks have more restrictive financing.
