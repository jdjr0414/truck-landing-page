Zero Down Truck Financing: No Down Payment Options

Zero down truck financing lets qualified borrowers finance a commercial truck without a down payment. Lenders that offer 100% financing typically require strong credit (720+) and an established business. This guide covers who qualifies, what to expect, and how zero down compares to putting money down. See truck down payment requirements and typical down payments.

Key Takeaways

  • Strong credit (720+) and established businesses often qualify for $0 down
  • Zero down means higher monthly payments and more interest over the loan
  • Some lenders offer 100% financing on new equipment for prime borrowers

Who Qualifies for Zero Down Truck Financing?

Lenders that offer zero down or 100% financing typically require strong credit and a proven business. Personal credit 720+ is common. Time in business: often 2+ years with steady revenue. Some lenders work with 680+ for minimal down (5–10%). New businesses and lower credit usually need 15–30% down. See credit score requirements.

Zero Down: Trade-offs

Financing 100% means you borrow the full purchase price. Monthly payments are higher than with 10–20% down. Example: a $150,000 semi at 9% over 72 months. With $0 down: ~$2,700/month. With $30,000 (20%) down: ~$2,160/month. You also pay more interest over the life of the loan. Zero down preserves cash for operations but increases total cost. Use the truck loan calculator to compare scenarios.

Equipment Types and Zero Down

Zero down is most common for new equipment with strong resale value: semi tractors, dump trucks, box trucks. Used equipment often requires 15–25% down because of higher depreciation risk. Specialty equipment (bucket trucks, vac trucks) may have stricter advance rates. See semi truck financing and dump truck financing for vehicle-specific options.

What Lenders Evaluate for Zero Down

  • Credit score: 720+ preferred for 100% financing
  • Time in business: 2+ years typical
  • Revenue: Steady, documented income
  • Equipment: New or low-mileage used with strong collateral value
  • Existing debt: Debt-to-income and existing liens

Gather tax returns, bank statements, P&L, and equipment details. Pre-approval can confirm whether you qualify for zero down before you shop. See how to get pre-approved.

Common Questions

Can I get truck financing with zero down?

Yes. Strong credit (720+) and established businesses often qualify. Some lenders offer 100% financing for prime borrowers.

What credit score do I need for zero down truck financing?

Typically 720+ for best zero-down programs. Some work with 680+ for 5–10% down.

Does zero down mean higher monthly payments?

Yes. No down payment means a larger loan and higher monthly payments. Use the loan calculator to compare.

Can startups get zero down truck financing?

Rarely. Startups usually need 20–30% down. Established businesses with strong credit have better odds.
