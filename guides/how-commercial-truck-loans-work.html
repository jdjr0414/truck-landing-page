How Commercial Truck Loans Work

Commercial truck loans are secured by the truck. The lender holds a lien on the title until the loan is paid. Payments are typically fixed monthly. Interest rates depend on credit, equipment type, age, and term. Most lenders require the truck to be used primarily for business. Loan Structure

  • Secured: Truck secures the loan. Lien on title until paid.
  • Fixed payments: Monthly payments over term.
  • Terms: 24–84 months (new: 60–84; used: 24–60).
  • Rates: Typically 7–15% APR. Promotional rates on new dealer purchases.

What Lenders Evaluate

Business credit, revenue, time in business, equipment age/condition, and down payment. Strong applications qualify for better terms.

Loan vs Lease

Loans provide ownership; leases provide use. Equipment leases may offer different tax treatment. Compare both for your situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do commercial truck loans work?

Commercial truck loans are secured by the truck. Lenders hold a lien until paid. Fixed monthly payments over 24–84 months. Equipment secures the loan.

What is the typical term for a commercial truck loan?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage.

What rates do commercial truck loans have?

Rates typically range from 7% to 15% APR for qualified borrowers. Promotional rates may be lower on new dealer purchases.
