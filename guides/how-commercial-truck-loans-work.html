How Commercial Truck Loans Work

Commercial truck loans are secured by the truck. The lender holds a lien on the title until the loan is paid. Payments are typically fixed monthly. Interest rates depend on credit, equipment type, age, and term. Most lenders require the truck to be used primarily for business. Key Takeaways

  • Financing terms commonly range from 24-84 months
  • Strong credit businesses may qualify with little or no down payment

What Lenders Evaluate

Business credit, revenue, time in business, equipment age/condition, and down payment. Strong applications qualify for better terms.

Advance rate (loan-to-value): Lenders cap the loan at a percentage of equipment value. New trucks: often 80–90%. Used: 70–85% depending on age and mileage. A $100k truck at 80% advance = $80k max loan; you need $20k down. See What is Advance Rate.

Collateral strength: Semi trucks and dump trucks have strong resale markets; lenders offer better advance rates and terms. Vocational equipment (bucket trucks, vac trucks) may have slightly lower advance rates due to narrower markets.

Lien and Title

The lender files a lien on the truck title. You hold the title but cannot sell or transfer without satisfying the loan. Once paid, the lender releases the lien. For dealer purchases, the lender may fund directly to the dealer; title and lien are recorded at closing.

Prepayment and Refinancing

Most commercial truck loans allow prepayment without penalty. Refinancing is possible when rates drop or credit improves. See Truck Loan Refinancing. Some promotional dealer loans may have prepayment restrictions–read the contract.

Loan vs Lease

Loans provide ownership; leases provide use. Equipment leases may offer different tax treatment. Compare both for your situation.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need for commercial truck loans?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for best terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments.

How much down payment do commercial truck loans require?

Typically 10–30%. New trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with $0 down.

Can startup trucking companies get truck loans?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or load history. First-time buyers may face stricter terms.

How long are commercial truck loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage. Terms are tied to equipment useful life.

How fast can I get a commercial truck loan approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Captive lenders may offer same-day pre-approval for dealer purchases.

Can I get a loan for a used commercial truck?

Yes. Used truck loans are widely available. Terms are typically 24–60 months. Advance rates cap at 70–80% for used vs 80–90% for new.

What documentation do I need for a truck loan?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details (invoice or listing). Some programs require fewer documents.

What do commercial truck loans cost?

Rates typically range from 7% to 15% APR for qualified borrowers. Promotional rates may be lower on new dealer purchases. Cost depends on credit and term.
