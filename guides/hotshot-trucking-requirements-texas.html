Definition

Hotshot trucking in Texas follows federal FMCSA rules plus Texas state registration. Texas is a major hub for oilfield, equipment, and manufacturing freight. Requirements include USDOT (interstate), CDL if over weight thresholds, and Texas apportioned registration (IRP) for heavier rigs.

Federal Requirements (Apply in Texas)

Federal rules apply regardless of state. CDL: GCWR over 26,001 lbs with trailer over 10,000 lbs requires Class A CDL. USDOT: Required for interstate commerce. MC authority: Required for for-hire hauling. See DOT registration requirements and CDL vs non-CDL hotshot.

Texas-Specific Requirements

Texas participates in IRP and IFTA. If your rig is 26,001+ lbs GVWR and you operate interstate, you register through Texas as your base state. Texas weight fees and registration vary by vehicle. Intrastate-only operations may have different rules. Verify with commercial vehicle registration Texas and Texas DPS.

Common Questions

Do I need a CDL for hotshot in Texas?

If GCWR under 26,001 lbs and trailer under 10,000 lbs, you may not need a CDL in Texas. Over those thresholds requires Class A CDL.

What registration do I need for hotshot in Texas?

USDOT for interstate. Texas apportioned registration (IRP) if 26,001+ lbs. IFTA for fuel tax if interstate.

Does Texas require USDOT for hotshot?

Yes, for interstate commerce. Intrastate-only may have different rules—verify with Texas DPS.

Can I run hotshot in Texas without IRP?

If your rig is under 26,001 lbs GVWR and you operate interstate, you may not need IRP. Verify with Texas DPS.

What insurance do I need for Texas hotshot?

Primary liability (required), cargo insurance (for-hire), physical damage (if financed). Brokers often require $1M+ liability. See hotshot trucking insurance requirements.

How do I get a Texas base plate for hotshot?

Through Texas DPS. If 26,001+ lbs and interstate, apply for IRP through Texas as base state.
