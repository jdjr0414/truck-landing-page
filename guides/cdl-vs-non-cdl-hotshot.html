CDL vs Non-CDL Hotshot: Requirements Explained

Hotshot trucking may or may not require a CDL depending on GVWR and GCWR. The federal rule: combination over 26,001 lbs with trailer over 10,000 lbs requires Class A CDL. Many hotshot operators stay under to avoid CDL. This guide explains the rules and state variations. See CDL Class A vs Class B.

Key Takeaways

  • GCWR under 26,001 lbs + trailer under 10,000 lbs: often no CDL
  • Over 26,001 lbs GCWR: Class A CDL required
  • State rules vary—verify with state DMV

CDL vs non-CDL hotshot: Federal rule—GCWR over 26,001 lbs with trailer over 10,000 lbs requires Class A CDL. Under those limits, CDL may not be required in some states. State rules vary. See GCWR.

Definition

Non-CDL hotshot refers to operating a pickup and gooseneck trailer combination that stays under federal CDL weight thresholds (26,001 lbs GCWR, trailer under 10,000 lbs GVWR). CDL hotshot exceeds those limits and requires a Class A commercial driver's license. See CDL Class A vs Class B.

The 26,001 lb Rule

Federal CDL requirements: A Class A CDL is required when the combination GVWR exceeds 26,001 lbs and the trailer GVWR exceeds 10,000 lbs. Many hotshot setups (one-ton pickup + gooseneck) exceed 26,001 lbs GCWR, so CDL is required. Some operators spec lighter rigs to stay under.

Non-CDL Hotshot

To run without a CDL, you must stay under 26,001 lbs GCWR and trailer under 10,000 lbs GVWR. That limits payload—typically under 15,000 lbs total. Lighter loads, equipment parts, smaller machinery. Many hotshot loads exceed this, so CDL is common.

State Variations

State rules can be stricter than federal. Some states require CDL at lower weights for intrastate operations. Verify with your state DMV and FMCSA before operating. See commercial truck license requirements.

Common Questions

Do I need a CDL for hotshot trucking?

If GCWR (truck + trailer + cargo) is under 26,001 lbs and trailer GVWR under 10,000 lbs, you may not need a CDL in some states. Over those thresholds requires Class A CDL.

What is the 26,001 lb rule?

Federal CDL requirement: combination GVWR over 26,001 lbs with trailer over 10,000 lbs requires Class A CDL. Many hotshot rigs exceed this.

Can I run hotshot without a CDL?

Yes, if you stay under 26,001 lbs GCWR and trailer under 10,000 lbs. State rules vary—verify with your state DMV.

What payload can I haul without a CDL?

Typically under 15,000 lbs total. Depends on truck + trailer + cargo staying under 26,001 lbs GCWR and trailer under 10,000 lbs GVWR.

Do I need an ELD for non-CDL hotshot?

ELD is required for CDL drivers. Non-CDL operators may use paper logs or electronic logs depending on state. Verify with FMCSA.

Is CDL worth it for hotshot?

CDL opens access to heavier loads and more freight. Training cost $3,000–$7,000. Many hotshot loads exceed non-CDL limits, so CDL is common.
