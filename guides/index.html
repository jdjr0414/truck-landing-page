Financing Process Overview

The typical flow: gather business documentation (tax returns, bank statements, P&L) and equipment details; compare lenders (banks, credit unions, specialty commercial lenders); submit application; receive approval (often 24–72 hours for qualified applicants); close and fund. Down payment typically 10–30%; strong credit may qualify for $0 down. Terms: 36–84 months for new equipment, 24–60 months for used depending on age and mileage.

By Borrower Type

Owner-operators: See Owner-Operator Financing. Lenders may require carrier agreements, load history, or proof of contracts. Down payments often 15–25% for new operators.

Fleet managers: See Fleet Financing Guide. Multi-unit programs may offer volume pricing. Documentation requirements scale with fleet size.

Contractors: See Equipment Financing for Contractors. Construction, utility, and environmental contractors have equipment-specific programs.

Startups: See Startup Trucking Business Financing. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts, and business plan. Some specialty lenders focus on new businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for truck financing?

Typical documents include business tax returns (2 years), profit and loss statements, bank statements, equipment quote or invoice, and driver's license. For startups, lenders may require contracts or proof of revenue. See Documents Needed for Truck Financing for details.

How long does truck financing approval take?

Pre-approval often takes 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: 1–5 business days for straightforward applications. Complex deals (startups, multiple units) may take 1–2 weeks. See How Fast Can You Get Truck Financing.

Can I finance a used truck?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available for semi trucks, dump trucks, bucket trucks, vac trucks, tow trucks, box trucks, and more. Terms typically 24–60 months depending on age and mileage. See New vs Used Truck Financing for details.

What credit score is needed for truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates; 720+ often qualifies for the best terms. Some specialty lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments. See Credit Score Requirements.
