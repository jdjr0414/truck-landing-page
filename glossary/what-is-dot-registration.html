What Is DOT Registration?

DOT registration is the process of registering a commercial motor carrier with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). It typically involves obtaining a USDOT number–a unique identifier assigned to track commercial vehicles and carriers. DOT registration is required for interstate commerce and often for intrastate operations depending on state rules.

Key Takeaways

  • USDOT number is required for interstate commercial motor carriers
  • Intrastate requirements vary by state–many require DOT for heavy vehicles
  • Register through FMCSA Unified Registration System (no fee)
  • Processing typically takes 1–2 business days

Quick Answer

DOT registration means obtaining a USDOT number from the FMCSA. It is required for commercial motor vehicles used in interstate commerce. Intrastate-only operations depend on state rules–many states require DOT registration for vehicles over 26,000 lbs GVWR. Register online at the FMCSA Unified Registration System. No fee.

When Is DOT Registration Required?

  • Commercial motor vehicles in interstate commerce
  • Vehicles 10,001+ lbs GVWR used in interstate commerce
  • Vehicles transporting 9+ passengers for compensation
  • Hazmat vehicles requiring placards
  • Many states require DOT for intrastate heavy vehicles (e.g., 26,000+ lbs GVWR)

USDOT Number vs MC Number

USDOT number identifies the carrier for safety and compliance. MC (Motor Carrier) number is required for carriers hauling freight for hire in interstate commerce. Both are obtained through FMCSA registration.
