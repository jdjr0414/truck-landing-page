What Is a Hazmat Endorsement?

A hazmat endorsement (H endorsement) is an add-on to a CDL that allows you to haul hazardous materials. It requires a TSA security threat assessment, a knowledge test, and compliance with federal hazmat regulations. Tanker haulers, chemical haulers, and certain oilfield carriers often need it.

Key Takeaways

  • Hazmat (H) endorsement allows hauling hazardous materials
  • Requires TSA background check and CDL knowledge test
  • Common for tanker, chemical, and oilfield hauling
  • Must be renewed; TSA check required every 5 years

Who Needs a Hazmat Endorsement

Drivers hauling flammable liquids, gases, explosives, corrosives, or other hazardous materials in quantities that require placarding. Tanker trucking, chemical hauling, fuel delivery, and certain oilfield loads often require an H or X endorsement.
