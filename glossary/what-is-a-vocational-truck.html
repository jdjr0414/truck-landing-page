What Is a Vocational Truck?

A vocational truck is a work-specific truck built for a particular job. Unlike over-the-road tractors that mainly pull trailers, vocational trucks have specialized bodies and equipment: dump bodies, aerial lifts, vacuum tanks, tow equipment, refuse bodies, and more. They are the backbone of construction, utilities, waste, and service industries.

Key Takeaways

  • Vocational truck = work-specific truck for a particular job
  • Examples: dump, bucket, vac, tow, garbage, street sweeper
  • Often Class 6–8; many require CDL Class B
  • Financing available through commercial equipment lenders
