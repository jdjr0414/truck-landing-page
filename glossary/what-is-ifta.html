What Is IFTA?

IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement) is a system that simplifies fuel tax reporting for commercial vehicles that operate in multiple states or Canadian provinces. Instead of buying fuel permits in each jurisdiction, you file one quarterly return with your base state.

Key Takeaways

  • IFTA = International Fuel Tax Agreement
  • Simplifies fuel tax for interstate trucking
  • One quarterly return instead of permits in each state
  • Required for vehicles over 26,000 lbs GVWR or 26,000 lbs GCWR

Quick Answer

IFTA is the International Fuel Tax Agreement. It lets interstate truckers file one quarterly fuel tax return with their base state instead of buying fuel permits in every state they travel through. Applies to vehicles over 26,000 lbs GVWR or GCWR.

Who Needs IFTA

Commercial vehicles with a gross vehicle weight or gross combination weight of 26,001 lbs or more that travel in two or more IFTA member jurisdictions. Most interstate trucking operations need IFTA. Intrastate-only carriers may not.

IFTA vs. IRP

IFTA covers fuel taxes. IRP (International Registration Plan) covers vehicle registration and license plates for apportioned vehicles. Both apply to interstate commercial carriers.

How IFTA Works

You register for IFTA with your base state (where your vehicles are registered). You receive IFTA decals and a license. Each quarter you report miles traveled and fuel purchased in each jurisdiction. Your base state calculates tax owed or refund due and distributes funds to other states. Filing is typically due by the last day of the month following each quarter (April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31).

IFTA and Truck Financing

IFTA registration is a regulatory requirement for interstate trucking–it does not directly affect financing approval. Lenders may verify that you have proper authority (DOT, MC, IFTA) when financing trucks for freight hauling. See What is DOT Registration and What is MC Number.

Common Questions

Do I need IFTA for intrastate trucking?

No. IFTA applies to vehicles that travel in two or more IFTA member jurisdictions. Intrastate-only carriers typically do not need IFTA.

When are IFTA returns due?

Quarterly. Due by the last day of the month following each quarter: April 30 (Q1), July 31 (Q2), October 31 (Q3), January 31 (Q4).

Does IFTA apply to vocational trucks?

Yes, if the vehicle exceeds 26,000 lbs GVWR or GCWR and travels in multiple states. Dump trucks, bucket trucks, and other vocational trucks that cross state lines may need IFTA.
