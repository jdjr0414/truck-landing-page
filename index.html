Strategy

Organize by truck type first

Truck-type pages usually capture stronger intent than industry-only pages. For trucks that span multiple sectors, like vac and bucket trucks, use industry pages as support content linking back to the main truck pages.

Primary pages

Semi trucks, dump trucks, bucket trucks, vac trucks, box trucks, tow trucks, service trucks, and fleet financing.

Support pages

Construction, utility contractors, environmental services, septic, oilfield, logistics, and municipal use cases.

Conversion flow

Every page should route inquiries to one intake path tied back to Axiant Partners.
