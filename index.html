Equipment Questions

What commercial trucks can I finance? Semi trucks, dump trucks, bucket trucks, vac trucks, tow trucks, box trucks, crane trucks, fire trucks, garbage trucks, cement trucks, flatbeds, tankers, refrigerated trucks, street sweepers, hydro excavation trucks, and more. See vehicle pages.

What is a vocational truck? Trucks built for specific work: bucket (aerial), vac (vacuum excavation), dump (hauling), tow (recovery). Contrast with over-the-road tractors.

Cost Questions

How much do commercial trucks cost? Semi: $120K–$200K new. Dump: $80K–$180K. Bucket: $90K–$250K. Vac: $150K–$350K. Box: $35K–$80K. See average cost data.

How much down payment? Typically 10–30%. Strong credit: often $0 down. Startups: 20–30%. See typical down payments.

Startup Questions

Can startups get truck financing? Yes. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts or revenue, and a business plan. Some lenders specialize in startups. See can startups get financing.

Financing Questions

How do commercial truck loans work? Secured loans; lender holds lien. Terms 36–84 months. Rates vary by credit and equipment. See how truck loans work.

How fast is approval? Application review: same day. Documentation: 24–48 hours. Funding: often within a few days. See financing timeline.

Credit Questions

What credit score for truck financing? 650+ for competitive terms. 720+ for best rates. Some lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments. See credit requirements.

Industry Questions

Which industries use truck financing? Trucking, construction, utilities, environmental services, waste management, municipal, telecommunications, agriculture, oilfield. See industry pages.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need for commercial truck financing?

Many programs require 650+ for competitive terms. Some specialty lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments.

How much down payment is required?

Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with $0 down.

Can startup businesses get truck financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with startups. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.

How long are commercial truck loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage.

How fast can I get truck financing approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Complex deals: 1–2 weeks. Pre-approval can speed the process.

Can I finance used commercial trucks?

Yes. Used truck financing is widely available for semi trucks, dump trucks, bucket trucks, vac trucks, tow trucks, box trucks, and more. Terms are typically 24–60 months.

What documentation do I need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details (invoice, spec sheet, or listing).

What do commercial trucks typically cost?

Semi trucks: $120,000–$200,000. Dump trucks: $80,000–$180,000. Bucket trucks: $90,000–$250,000. Vac trucks: $150,000–$350,000. Tow trucks: $60,000–$130,000. Box trucks: $35,000–$80,000. Garbage trucks: $200,000–$400,000. Fire trucks: $300,000–$1M+. Costs vary by type and condition.

Can I finance a semi truck?

Yes. Semi truck financing is widely available for tractors (day cab and sleeper). New: $120,000–$200,000. Used: $50,000–$120,000. See semi truck financing.

Can I finance a dump truck?

Yes. Dump truck financing covers standard, tri-axle, and heavy-duty units. New: $80,000–$180,000. Used: $45,000–$90,000. See dump truck financing.

Can I finance a bucket truck?

Yes. Bucket truck financing covers aerial work platforms for utilities, tree care, and telecom. New: $90,000–$250,000. Used: $40,000–$120,000. See bucket truck financing.

Can I finance a vac truck or hydro excavation truck?

Yes. Vac truck and hydro excavation financing is available for environmental services and utility contractors. New: $150,000–$350,000. See vac truck financing and hydro excavation financing.

Can I finance a tow truck?

Yes. Tow truck financing covers flatbed, wheel-lift, integrated, and heavy-duty recovery. New: $60,000–$150,000. See tow truck financing.

Can I finance a garbage truck or fire truck?

Yes. Garbage truck financing ($200,000–$400,000) and fire truck financing ($300,000–$1M+) are available. Municipal entities often use lease-purchase. See garbage truck financing and fire truck financing.

Can I finance a crane truck, flatbed, or box truck?

Yes. Crane trucks ($100,000–$300,000), flatbed trucks ($80,000–$150,000), and box trucks ($35,000–$80,000) are all commonly financed. See crane truck, flatbed, and box truck financing.
