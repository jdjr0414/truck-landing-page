Common Questions

What credit score do I need for semi truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Owner-operators often need 20–30% down.

How much down payment for a semi truck?

Typically 10–30%. New tractors often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit can reduce requirements.

Can startup owner-operators get semi truck financing?

Yes. Some lenders work with new owner-operators. Expect 20–30% down and proof of contracts or carrier agreements.

How long are semi truck loan terms?

New tractors: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and mileage.

How fast can semi truck financing be approved?

Simple applications: 1–3 business days. Dealer purchases may have same-day pre-approval.

Can I finance a used semi truck?

Yes. Used semi truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

What documentation do I need?

Business tax returns, bank statements, P&L, driver's license, and equipment details. Owner-operators need proof of contracts.

What do semi trucks cost?

New: $120,000–$200,000. Used: $40,000–$120,000. Day cabs cost less than sleepers.
