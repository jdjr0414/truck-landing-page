Commercial equipment financing covers loans secured by business equipment—trucks, trailers, machinery, and vocational vehicles. Lenders hold a lien on the equipment until the loan is paid. This guide explains how equipment financing works, what lenders evaluate, and how to structure deals for new and used equipment.

What Is Commercial Equipment Financing?

Commercial equipment financing is lending secured by equipment used for business purposes. The lender holds a lien on the equipment until the loan is paid. If you default, the lender can repossess the equipment. Unlike unsecured business loans, equipment financing uses the equipment as collateral, which often allows lower rates and longer terms for qualified borrowers.

Typical Cost Range

Equipment costs vary widely. Semi trucks range from $120,000 to $200,000+ new. Dump trucks: $80,000–$150,000. Bucket trucks: $150,000–$400,000+. Advance rates typically cap at 80–90% for new and 70–80% for used. See how much does a semi truck cost and average cost of commercial trucks for detailed pricing.

Average Down Payment

Down payments typically range from 10% to 30%. New equipment from dealers often allows 10–15% for qualified borrowers. Used equipment may require 20–30%. Owner-operators and startups often need 20–30%. See how much down for semi truck for more.

Common Industries

  • Construction and contracting
  • Trucking and freight
  • Utility contracting
  • Environmental services
  • Waste management
  • Municipal and public works

Typical Business Profile

Lenders prefer 12–24 months in business minimum; stronger programs require 2+ years. Annual revenue should support the payment. Personal credit 650+ typically qualifies for competitive terms. Some programs work with lower credit and higher down payments.

Equipment Loans vs. Leases

Equipment loans provide ownership; you own the equipment after the final payment. Equipment leases provide use; the lessor retains ownership. Leases may offer different tax treatment. Compare both for your situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is commercial equipment financing?

Commercial equipment financing is lending secured by business equipment—trucks, trailers, machinery—used for business purposes. The equipment secures the loan.

What is the typical down payment for equipment financing?

Down payments typically range from 10% to 30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%; used equipment may require 20–30%.

How long can I finance commercial equipment?

New equipment: 60–84 months. Used equipment: 24–60 months depending on age and useful life.

What credit score do I need for equipment financing?

Many programs require 650+ for competitive terms. Some lenders work with 580–650 with higher down payments.

Can I finance used commercial equipment?

Yes. Used equipment financing is widely available. Terms are typically shorter than for new equipment.
