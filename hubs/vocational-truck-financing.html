Financing for Bucket Trucks, Vac Trucks, Service Trucks & Utility Vehicles

Vocational trucks are work trucks built for specific trades: bucket trucks for utilities and tree care, vac trucks for excavation and environmental services, service trucks for field repair, and utility trucks for contractors. This guide covers how to finance these vehicles.

What Are Vocational Trucks?

Vocational trucks are chassis with specialized bodies or equipment for a specific trade. Unlike standard hauling trucks, they're configured for work at height, excavation, field service, or utility operations. Common types include bucket trucks, vac trucks, service trucks, crane trucks, and utility trucks. Financing considers both the chassis and the vocational equipment.

Bucket Truck Financing

Bucket truck financing covers aerial work platforms mounted on truck chassis. Used by utilities, tree care, telecom, and municipal crews. Lenders evaluate aerial reach, capacity, and condition. New bucket trucks range from $150,000 to $400,000+; used units are widely financed. Terms typically run 60–84 months for new and 36–60 months for used. See also telecom bucket truck financing for communications-specific units.

Vac Truck Financing

Vac truck financing covers vacuum excavation and industrial cleaning trucks. Used by environmental services, utilities, septic, and oilfield support. Vac trucks include hydro excavation units, septic vacs, and industrial vacuum trucks. Financing considers tank capacity, vacuum specs, and chassis. Specialty units like septic vac trucks and hydro excavation trucks may have niche lender programs.

Service Truck Financing

Service truck financing covers trucks with service bodies, tool storage, and work equipment. Used by mobile repair, field service, contractors, and utilities. Service bodies add $15,000–$50,000+ to chassis cost. Lenders finance the complete unit. Utility truck financing overlaps for utility contractor fleets.

Crane and Boom Truck Financing

Crane truck financing and boom truck financing cover trucks with mounted cranes or material handlers. Used in construction, logistics, and material handling. Crane capacity and reach affect value. Lenders familiar with construction equipment offer better terms.

Fire and Municipal Vocational Trucks

Fire truck financing and ladder truck financing serve municipal and industrial fire departments. These are high-value specialty units with longer useful life. Municipal financing may involve different structures and approval processes.

Financing Considerations

  • Chassis + body: Lenders finance the complete unit. Ensure the body and equipment are properly documented.
  • Age and condition: Vocational equipment depreciates. Well-maintained units retain value better.
  • Industry: Lenders familiar with your industry may offer better terms.
  • Down payment: Typically 10–30%. New equipment often allows 10–15%.

Who Uses Vocational Trucks?

Utility contractors, tree care companies, telecom installers, environmental services, septic haulers, oilfield support, municipal departments, and field service businesses. Many vocational trucks cross industries—bucket trucks serve utilities and tree care; vac trucks serve environmental and utility work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vocational truck?

A vocational truck is a work truck equipped for a specific trade: bucket trucks for utilities, vac trucks for excavation, service trucks for field repair, etc.

How do I finance a bucket truck?

Bucket truck financing follows standard equipment lending. Lenders evaluate business credit, revenue, and the truck's age, condition, and aerial specs.

Can I finance a used vac truck?

Yes. Used vac truck financing is available. Terms are typically shorter than for new equipment.

What down payment is needed for vocational trucks?

Typically 10–30% depending on credit, equipment age, and lender. New units often allow 10–15%.
