What Are Heavy Duty Trucks?

Heavy duty trucks are Class 7 (26,001–33,000 lbs GVWR) and Class 8 (33,001+ lbs GVWR) commercial vehicles. They include semi tractors, dump trucks, cement mixers, garbage trucks, tankers, heavy haul, and logging trucks. These units have the highest acquisition costs and strongest resale markets in commercial trucking.

Semi Truck Financing

Semi truck financing covers tractors for freight hauling. Day cabs and sleepers are both financed. New tractors range from $120,000 to $200,000+; used units are widely available. Terms typically run 60–84 months for new and 36–60 months for used. See day cab semi financing and sleeper truck financing for cab-specific programs.

Dump Truck Financing

Dump truck financing covers trucks for construction, aggregate, and materials hauling. Dump trucks range from $80,000 to $150,000+ new. Strong resale markets support competitive financing. Lenders consider haul contracts and revenue.

Cement Truck Financing

Cement truck financing covers ready-mix trucks and mixer trucks. Used by concrete suppliers and contractors. Cement trucks are specialized; lenders familiar with construction equipment offer appropriate programs.

Heavy Haul Truck Financing

Heavy haul truck financing covers specialized tractors and trailers for oversized loads. Used by heavy haul carriers and specialized logistics. Heavy haul equipment can exceed $300,000; financing considerations include equipment specs and haul revenue.

Logging and Tanker Truck Financing

Logging truck financing and tanker truck financing cover specialized hauling equipment. Logging trucks have regional markets; tankers serve liquid hauling. Both have niche lenders familiar with the equipment.

Garbage Truck Financing

Garbage truck financing covers refuse trucks for waste and recycling. Municipal and private haulers use these units. Garbage trucks are high-value; terms may extend to 84+ months for new equipment.

Financing Terms for Heavy Duty Trucks

  • New trucks: 60–84 months, 10–15% down, competitive rates.
  • Used trucks: 24–60 months, 20–30% down, higher rates.
  • Advance rates: 80–90% for new, 70–80% for used.
  • Rates: Typically 7–15% APR depending on credit and equipment.

Who Finances Heavy Duty Trucks?

Banks, credit unions, captive finance companies (PACCAR, Navistar, etc.), and specialty commercial lenders. Captive lenders often offer promotional rates on new dealer purchases. Axiant Partners matches businesses with heavy duty truck financing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a heavy duty truck?

Heavy duty trucks are Class 7 and Class 8 commercial trucks: semi tractors, dump trucks, cement mixers, etc.

How much can I finance for a heavy duty truck?

Heavy duty trucks can be financed from $50,000 to $200,000+ depending on type. Advance rates typically cap at 80–90% for new and 70–80% for used.

What terms are available for heavy duty trucks?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used trucks: 24–60 months depending on age and mileage.

Do I need a CDL to finance a heavy duty truck?

Lenders typically don't require a CDL for financing, but they may verify business use and operator qualifications.
