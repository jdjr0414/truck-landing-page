How the truck financing calculator works

It uses standard loan amortization — the same math a lender uses:

  • Amount financed = truck price − down payment.
  • Monthly payment is calculated from the amount financed, the interest rate, and the number of months.
  • Total interest = total of all payments − amount financed.

A bigger down payment or a shorter term lowers your total interest; a longer term lowers the monthly payment but costs more overall. Rates vary with credit, time in business, and the age of the truck — newer trucks and stronger credit earn the lowest rates.

What affects your truck loan

Lenders price commercial truck loans on the truck (year, mileage, type), your credit and time in business, and your down payment. Owner-operators just starting out typically see higher rates and larger down payments than established fleets. Financing the right truck on terms that fit your revenue keeps the payment manageable between loads.

Truck financing questions

How much does it cost to finance a commercial truck?

Your payment depends on price, down payment, rate, and term. Financing $120,000 over 60 months at 8.5% with $15,000 down is about $2,154/month. Use the calculator for your own numbers.

What rate can I expect?

Commercial truck rates commonly run 6%–18%, depending on credit, time in business, truck age, and down payment.

How much down payment do I need?

Typically 0%–25%. Established fleets may qualify for 0–10% down; first-time owner-operators or used trucks often need 10–25%.

What term is best?

Usually 36–72 months. Longer terms lower the monthly payment but raise total interest. Match the term to how long you'll keep the truck.
