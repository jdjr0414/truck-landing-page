Who Needs Bucket Truck Financing?

Bucket truck financing serves utility contractors, tree care companies, telecom installers, electrical contractors, and municipal departments. These businesses need aerial access for line work, tree trimming, antenna installation, and maintenance. Bucket trucks range from 35-foot to 65-foot+ working height. Lenders evaluate the combined value of chassis and aerial device.

Aerial Specs and Valuation

Bucket truck value depends on working height, platform capacity, and chassis. A 55-foot over-center aerial has different value than a 35-foot rear-mount. Lenders familiar with utility equipment understand these specs. Document the aerial manufacturer, model, and reach. Well-maintained aerials retain value—inspect boom condition and hydraulic systems before purchase.

New vs. Used Bucket Truck Financing

New bucket trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down for qualified borrowers. Used bucket truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Aerial condition matters—lenders may require inspection reports for older units. Telecom bucket truck financing covers units configured for communications work; terms are similar.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Business revenue: Utility contracts, tree care revenue, or telecom work. Steady revenue supports approval.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Chassis age/mileage, aerial specs, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit affect rate and approval.

Related Equipment

Utility truck financing covers utility contractor fleets including bucket trucks and support vehicles. Service truck financing covers trucks with service bodies—often used alongside bucket trucks. Crane truck financing and boom truck financing cover material-handling booms. Vac truck financing covers vacuum excavation—some utility work uses both bucket and vac trucks.

How much does a bucket truck typically cost?

New bucket trucks: $90,000–$250,000. Used: $40,000–$120,000. See how much does a bucket truck cost.

What credit score is needed for bucket truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for best terms.

Can businesses qualify with no down payment?

Yes. Strong credit and established businesses often qualify with $0 down. Down payments are risk-based.

How long are bucket truck financing terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and aerial condition.

How quickly can bucket truck financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days.

Can I finance a used bucket truck?

Yes. Used bucket truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.
