Airport Fire Truck Financing

Financing for Airport Fire Trucks & ARFF Vehicles

Airport fire truck financing covers ARFF (Aircraft Rescue and Firefighting) vehicles used at airports. ARFF trucks meet FAA requirements for aircraft fire response and are required for Part 139 certificated airports. These are high-value specialty vehicles—new units often exceed $1M. Municipal and airport financing structures apply.
