Street Sweeper Truck Financing

Financing for Street Sweepers & Municipal Sweepers

Street sweeper truck financing covers mechanical and vacuum sweepers used by municipalities and contractors. Street sweepers clean roads, parking lots, and industrial sites. Municipal entities often use lease-purchase or municipal financing. Private contractors use standard equipment loans. Both new and used street sweepers are financed.

Types of Street Sweepers

Mechanical sweepers use rotating brooms to collect debris. Vacuum sweepers use vacuum systems for finer material. Both types are financed. Municipal sweepers often have longer useful life—terms may extend to 84+ months for new equipment.

Who Needs Street Sweeper Financing?

Municipalities, street sweeping contractors, and industrial facilities. Revenue comes from municipal contracts, per-mile or per-lot fees, or facility maintenance contracts. Municipal entities may use different financing structures than private contractors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a street sweeper cost to finance?

Street sweepers range from $150,000 to $350,000+ depending on type. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Who uses street sweepers?

Municipalities, contractors, and industrial facilities. Street sweepers clean roads, parking lots, and industrial sites.

Can I finance a used street sweeper?

Yes. Used street sweeper financing is available. Terms are typically 36–72 months.

What types of street sweepers can I finance?

Mechanical sweepers (broom) and vacuum sweepers. Both are commonly financed.
