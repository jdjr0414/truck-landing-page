Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vacuum excavator?

A vacuum excavator uses vacuum to excavate soil for utility locate work. Similar to hydro excavation but may use air or water. Also called vac-ex.

How much does a vacuum excavator cost to finance?

Vacuum excavators range from $120,000 to $350,000+ depending on specs. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Vacuum excavator vs hydro excavation?

Both are non-destructive digging methods. Hydro excavation uses water; vacuum excavators may use air or water. Financing is similar.
