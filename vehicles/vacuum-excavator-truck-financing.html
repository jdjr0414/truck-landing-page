Common Questions

What credit score is needed to finance a vacuum excavator?

Most lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for the best terms. Utility and environmental contractors with project contracts may qualify with lower scores.

How much down payment is required for vacuum excavator financing?

Typically 10–30%. New units often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with little or no down payment.

Can startups finance vacuum excavators?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer utility or environmental contractors. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts, and strong personal credit.

How long do vacuum excavator loans usually last?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and vacuum specs. Financing structures similar to hydro excavation and vac trucks.

How quickly can vacuum excavator financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days. Have business documentation and equipment specs ready.

Can I finance a used vacuum excavator?

Yes. Used vacuum excavator financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Vacuum capacity and tank condition affect valuation.

What documents are needed for vacuum excavator financing?

Business tax returns (2 years), bank statements (3–6 months), driver's license, and equipment details (vacuum specs, tank capacity, price).

How much does a vacuum excavator cost to finance?

Vacuum excavators range from $120,000 to $350,000+ depending on specs. Down payments typically run 10–30%. Used for utility daylighting and locate work.

What is a vacuum excavator?

A vacuum excavator uses vacuum to excavate soil for utility locate work. Similar to hydro excavation but may use air or water. Also called vac-ex. Financing is similar to hydro excavation.
