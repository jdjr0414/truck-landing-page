Crane Truck Financing

Financing for Crane Trucks & Truck-Mounted Material Handlers

Crane truck financing covers trucks with mounted cranes for material handling. Used by construction contractors, utilities, logistics, and material handling operations. Crane trucks combine a chassis with a crane—lenders finance the complete unit. Boom truck financing covers similar equipment with slightly different configurations.

Who Needs Crane Truck Financing?

Construction contractors, utility contractors, logistics companies, and material handling operations. Crane trucks lift and place materials—concrete barriers, precast, steel, equipment. Revenue comes from project work, contracts, or per-lift fees. Lenders evaluate business revenue, time in business, and equipment value. Crane capacity and reach affect valuation.

Crane Specs and Valuation

Crane truck value depends on crane capacity (tonnage), reach, and chassis. Knuckle-boom cranes and articulated cranes are common. Lenders familiar with construction equipment understand crane specs. Document crane manufacturer, model, capacity, and chassis. Well-maintained cranes retain value.

New vs. Used Crane Truck Financing

New crane trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used crane truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Crane condition and chassis mileage affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older cranes.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Project revenue, contract work, or material handling fees.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Crane capacity, reach, chassis, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

Related Equipment

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a crane truck cost to finance?

Crane trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on crane capacity and chassis. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

What is the difference between a crane truck and boom truck?

Crane trucks and boom trucks are similar; both have truck-mounted cranes. Crane trucks may have higher capacity for heavy lifting.

Can I finance a used crane truck?

Yes. Used crane truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

Who uses crane trucks?

Construction contractors, utilities, logistics, and material handling. Crane trucks lift and place materials at job sites.
