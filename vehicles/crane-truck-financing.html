Crane Specs and Valuation

Crane truck value depends on crane capacity (tonnage), reach, and chassis. Knuckle-boom cranes and articulated cranes are common. Lenders familiar with construction equipment understand crane specs. Document crane manufacturer, model, capacity, and chassis. Well-maintained cranes retain value.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment. Higher-risk scenarios—startups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding credit—may require 20–30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business typically receive the best terms—often 10–15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15–25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20–30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.
  • Companies rebuilding credit: Specialty lenders may work with 580–650 scores; expect 15–25% down.

New vs. Used Crane Truck Financing

New crane trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used crane truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Crane condition and chassis mileage affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older cranes.

Common Questions

What credit score is needed to finance a crane truck?

Most lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for the best terms. Construction contractors with project revenue may qualify with lower scores.

How much down payment is required for crane truck financing?

Typically 10–30%. New crane trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with little or no down payment.

Can startups finance crane trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer construction or utility contractors. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts, and strong personal credit.

How long do crane truck loans usually last?

New crane trucks: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and crane condition. Crane capacity and reach affect terms.

How quickly can crane truck financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days. Have business documentation and crane specs ready.

Can I finance a used crane truck?

Yes. Used crane truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Crane condition and capacity affect valuation.

What documents are needed for crane truck financing?

Business tax returns (2 years), bank statements (3–6 months), driver's license, and equipment details (crane manufacturer, capacity, chassis, price).

How much does a crane truck cost to finance?

Crane trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on crane capacity and chassis. Down payments typically run 10–30%. See how much does a crane truck cost.

What is the difference between a crane truck and boom truck?

Crane trucks and boom trucks are similar; both have truck-mounted cranes. Crane trucks may have higher capacity for heavy lifting. Both are commonly financed.
