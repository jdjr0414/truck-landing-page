Logging Truck Financing

Financing for Logging Trucks & Timber Haul Vehicles

Logging truck financing covers trucks and trailers used to haul logs from forest to mill. Logging trucks have specialized configurations—bunks, stakes, load securement—and are used primarily in timber-producing regions. Both new and used logging trucks are financed by lenders familiar with regional timber markets.

Who Needs Logging Truck Financing?

Logging truck operators, timber haulers, and forestry contractors. Revenue comes from haul rates (per ton or per load) from mills or logging contractors. Logging markets are regional—Pacific Northwest, Southeast, Northeast. Lenders familiar with timber markets understand seasonal patterns and equipment valuation.

Logging Truck Configuration

Logging trucks typically use a tractor and trailer or a single-unit truck with trailer. The trailer has bunks (bunks for log placement), stakes, and load securement. Some configurations include self-loading equipment. Lenders finance the complete unit. Document chassis, trailer specs, and configuration for accurate valuation.

New vs. Used Logging Truck Financing

New logging trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used logging truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Regional resale markets affect valuation—logging trucks in active timber regions retain value better. Equipment condition and trailer specs matter.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Haul revenue, mill contracts, or logging contractor agreements.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Chassis, trailer configuration, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

Related Equipment

Semi truck financing covers tractors. Flatbed truck financing covers flatbeds for general hauling. Dump truck financing covers dump trucks—some logging operations use dump trucks for chip hauling. Heavy haul truck financing covers specialized tractors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a logging truck cost to finance?

Logging trucks range from $150,000 to $350,000+ depending on configuration. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used logging truck?

Yes. Used logging truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

What is a logging truck?

A logging truck has a specialized trailer or chassis for hauling logs from forest to mill. Often includes bunk, stakes, and load securement.

Where are logging trucks used?

Timber-producing regions in the Pacific Northwest, Southeast, and Northeast. Logging trucks have regional resale markets.
