Types of Tanker Trucks

Fuel tankers haul gasoline, diesel, or aviation fuel. Chemical tankers haul hazardous or non-hazardous chemicals. Food-grade tankers haul milk, water, or edible liquids. Bulk liquid tankers haul cement slurry or other industrial liquids. Tank configuration—compartments, insulation, heating—varies by product. Lenders evaluate tank specs, certification, and condition.

Who Needs Tanker Truck Financing?

Fuel distributors, chemical haulers, milk haulers, water haulers, and oilfield support companies. Revenue comes from haul rates, contracts, or delivery fees. Tanker trucks require specialized certification (e.g., hazmat) for certain products. Lenders evaluate business revenue, haul contracts, and equipment value.

New vs. Used Tanker Truck Financing

New tanker trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used tanker truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Tank condition, certification status, and chassis mileage affect valuation. Tanks must meet regulatory requirements for the product hauled.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Haul revenue, delivery contracts, or fuel distribution revenue.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Tank capacity, configuration, certification, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

Related Equipment

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tanker truck cost to finance?

Tanker trucks range from $100,000 to $300,000+ depending on capacity and configuration. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used tanker truck?

Yes. Used tanker truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

What do tanker trucks haul?

Fuel, chemicals, milk, water, cement slurry, and other liquids. Tank configuration depends on product hauled.

What do lenders look for in tanker truck financing?

Business revenue from hauling, contracts, time in business, credit, and tank condition/certification.
