Types of Fire Trucks Financed

Pumpers are the primary firefighting apparatus with pump, tank, and hose. Aerials have elevated platforms for high-rise access. Ladder trucks provide ground ladders and aerial ladders. Ladder truck financing is a specialized subset. Rescue vehicles carry extrication and technical rescue equipment. Tankers supply water in rural areas. All types are financed. Airport fire truck financing covers ARFF (aircraft rescue and firefighting) vehicles.

Who Needs Fire Truck Financing?

Municipal fire departments, industrial fire brigades, airport fire rescue, and volunteer fire departments. Municipal entities often use lease-purchase or municipal financing—terms may extend 10–15 years. Industrial and private entities use standard equipment financing. Lenders familiar with fire apparatus understand pump specs, tank capacity, and aerial reach.

New vs. Used Fire Truck Financing

New fire trucks qualify for long terms (84–180 months) and competitive rates. Used fire truck financing typically runs 60–120 months with higher rates. Pump condition, tank integrity, and aerial specs affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection and appraisal for specialty apparatus.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Entity type: Municipal, industrial, or private. Approval processes differ.
  • Revenue/budget: Municipal budgets or industrial revenue support payments.
  • Equipment: Type, pump capacity, tank size, aerial specs, and condition.
  • Credit: Municipal credit ratings or business credit.

Related Equipment

Ladder truck financing and airport fire truck financing are specialized subsets. Service truck financing covers support vehicles. Garbage truck financing covers other municipal equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a fire truck cost to finance?

Fire trucks range from $300,000 to $1M+ depending on type. Down payments and terms vary by lender and entity type.

Can municipalities finance fire trucks?

Yes. Municipal fire departments often use lease-purchase or municipal financing structures. Terms may extend 10–15 years.

Can I finance a used fire truck?

Yes. Used fire truck financing is available. Terms are typically shorter.

What types of fire trucks can I finance?

Pumpers, aerials, ladder trucks, rescue vehicles, and tankers. All are commonly financed.
