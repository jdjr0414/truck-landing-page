Frequently Asked Questions

What is a ladder truck?

A ladder truck has an aerial ladder for firefighting and rescue. Used by municipal and industrial fire departments for high-rise access.

How much does a ladder truck cost to finance?

Ladder trucks range from $800,000 to $1.5M+ depending on ladder reach. Terms may extend 10–15 years.

Ladder truck vs aerial?

Ladder trucks have telescoping ladders. Aerials may have platforms. Both are fire apparatus with aerial reach.
