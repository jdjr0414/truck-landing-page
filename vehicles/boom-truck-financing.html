Boom Truck Financing

Financing for Boom Trucks & Truck-Mounted Material Handlers

Boom truck financing covers trucks with mounted cranes or material-handling booms. Used by construction contractors, utilities, and logistics for lifting and placing materials. Boom trucks are similar to crane trucks—both have truck-mounted cranes. Lenders finance the complete unit including chassis and boom.

Who Needs Boom Truck Financing?

Construction contractors, utility contractors, and material handling operations. Boom trucks lift precast, steel, equipment, and materials. Revenue comes from project work, contracts, or per-lift fees. Lenders evaluate business revenue, time in business, and equipment value.

Boom Specs and Valuation

Boom truck value depends on crane capacity (tonnage), reach, and chassis. Knuckle-boom and articulated cranes are common. Document crane manufacturer, model, capacity, and chassis for accurate valuation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a boom truck?

A boom truck has a truck-mounted crane or material handler for lifting and placing materials. Used in construction and logistics.

Boom truck vs crane truck?

Boom trucks and crane trucks are similar. Both have truck-mounted cranes. Crane trucks may have higher capacity for heavy lifting.

How much does a boom truck cost to finance?

Boom trucks range from $70,000 to $220,000+ depending on crane capacity. Down payments typically run 10–30%.
