PACCAR Financial (Captive Financing)

PACCAR Financial is the captive finance arm for Peterbilt and Kenworth trucks. It offers retail financing and leasing through authorized Peterbilt dealers. Benefits include promotional rates on new equipment, streamlined approval for dealer purchases, and programs tailored to owner-operators and fleets. Captive programs typically favor new truck purchases; used truck financing may have stricter age and mileage limits. Compare PACCAR Financial with independent lenders—captive programs can be strong for new dealer purchases, while independents may offer more flexibility on used equipment or credit.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment through PACCAR Financial or independent lenders. Higher-risk scenarios—startups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding credit—may require 20–30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business often qualify for favorable terms—typically 10–15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15–25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20–30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.

New vs. Used Peterbilt Financing

New Peterbilt trucks qualify for longer terms (60–84 months), lower rates, and higher advance rates through PACCAR Financial. Promotional rates are often available on dealer purchases. Peterbilt's strong resale value can support favorable used truck financing. Used Peterbilt financing is more restrictive than new: shorter terms (36–60 months), higher rates, and lower advances. Lenders consider mileage, age, and remaining useful life.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Time in business: Most prefer 12–24 months minimum; stronger programs require 2+ years.
  • Revenue: Annual revenue and cash flow support the payment. Owner-operators may provide load history or contracts.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit affect rate and approval. Scores of 650+ typically qualify for competitive terms.
  • Equipment: Age, mileage, condition, and resale value. Peterbilt's premium resale can support better terms on used equipment.

Financing Terms

Terms run 60–84 months for new and 36–60 months for used. Rates typically range from 7% to 15% APR for qualified commercial borrowers. Down payments are risk-based—strong credit and established businesses may qualify with no down payment.

Mileage and Age Limits for Used Peterbilts

Lenders impose mileage and age limits on used Peterbilt financing. Peterbilt's strong resale value can support more favorable limits—some programs allow 7 years or 700,000 miles for qualified borrowers. Older or higher-mileage units may face shorter terms or higher rates. The Model 389 in particular holds value well in the used market due to owner-operator demand.

Operating Cost Considerations

Peterbilt trucks have operating costs similar to other premium Class 8 tractors. The Model 579's aerodynamic design supports fuel efficiency on long-haul routes. Budget for $2,000–$6,000 monthly fuel, $800–$2,500 insurance, and $500–$2,000 maintenance. Peterbilt's dealer network provides strong parts and service support nationwide.

Common Questions

How much does a Peterbilt truck typically cost?

New Peterbilt Model 579: $140,000–$200,000. Model 389 and 567 vary. Used: $55,000–$140,000 depending on model and mileage.

What credit score is needed for Peterbilt financing?

PACCAR Financial and independent lenders typically prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ qualifies for the best terms.

Does Peterbilt offer manufacturer financing?

Yes. PACCAR Financial provides captive financing for Peterbilt trucks purchased through authorized dealers.

How long are Peterbilt truck financing terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and mileage.

Can I finance a used Peterbilt?

Yes. Both PACCAR Financial and independent lenders finance used Peterbilt trucks within age and mileage limits.

Can I finance a Peterbilt as an owner-operator?

Yes. Owner-operators can finance Peterbilt trucks. Lenders may require proof of contracts, load history, or carrier agreements.

How quickly can Peterbilt financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days.
