Financing for Day Cab Semi Trucks & Regional Tractors

Day cab semi financing covers tractors without sleeper berths—used for regional routes, local delivery, drayage, and distribution. Day cab drivers typically return home daily. Day cabs often have lower acquisition costs than sleepers. Both new and used day cab semis are widely financed.

Who Needs Day Cab Semi Financing?

Regional carriers, local delivery fleets, drayage operators, and distribution companies. Day cab semis suit routes where drivers return home daily—typically 200–400 mile radius. Revenue comes from freight, delivery contracts, or drayage fees. Lenders evaluate business revenue, time in business, and equipment value.

Day Cab vs. Sleeper

Sleeper truck financing covers tractors with sleeper berths for long-haul. Day cabs have no sleeper—lower weight, lower cost, better maneuverability for local work. Financing structures are similar; day cabs often have slightly lower acquisition cost. Resale markets differ—sleepers command premium in long-haul markets; day cabs in regional markets.

New vs. Used Day Cab Semi Financing

New day cab semis qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used day cab semi financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Mileage and condition affect valuation. Day cabs used in regional service often have lower mileage than long-haul sleepers.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Freight revenue, delivery contracts, or drayage fees.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Chassis, engine specs, mileage, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.
