Important: Aircraft Financing vs. Truck Financing

Aircraft financing is different from truck financing. Agricultural aircraft are financed through aviation lenders, not typical commercial truck lenders. Key differences:

  • Lenders: Aviation lenders specialize in aircraft; commercial truck lenders focus on trucks and equipment.
  • Collateral: Aircraft loans use FAA registration and aircraft title; truck loans use state title and chattel mortgage.
  • Documentation: Aircraft financing requires FAA documentation, airworthiness, and aviation-specific paperwork.
  • Terms: Aircraft loan terms and structures may differ from truck loans.

How Aircraft Financing Differs from Truck Financing

Aircraft financing uses different lenders, documentation, and structures than truck financing.

  • Aviation lenders: Specialize in aircraft; evaluate pilot credentials and aircraft value.
  • FAA registration: Collateral is tied to FAA registration; lien recorded with FAA.
  • Documentation: Airworthiness certificate, registration, logbooks, and aviation-specific paperwork.
  • Appraisal: Aircraft appraisals differ from truck appraisals.

What Lenders Evaluate (Aircraft)

  • Pilot credentials: Commercial license, ag aircraft certification.
  • Aircraft: Make, model, hours, condition, airworthiness.
  • Revenue: Application revenue, contracts.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

For aircraft financing, work with aviation lenders or brokers who specialize in agricultural aircraft. Gather pilot credentials, aircraft details (make, model, hours, price), and proof of revenue. Aircraft financing is different from truck financing—ensure you use aviation-specific resources.

Licensing and Regulatory Requirements

Agricultural aircraft require a commercial pilot license and agricultural aircraft operator certification. FAA regulations apply. Pesticide applicator certification is typically required for commercial application.
