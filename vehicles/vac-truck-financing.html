Types of Vac Trucks

Vac trucks fall into several categories. Hydro excavation trucks use high-pressure water and vacuum for non-destructive digging—used by utilities and environmental contractors. Septic vac trucks haul liquid waste from septic systems. Industrial vacuum trucks handle dry or wet material in industrial settings. Hydro excavation truck financing and septic vac truck financing are specialized subsets with similar structures.

Who Needs Vac Truck Financing?

Environmental services firms, utility contractors, septic haulers, oilfield support companies, and industrial cleaning businesses. Revenue comes from excavation contracts, septic pumping, tank cleaning, or utility locate work. Lenders evaluate business stability, revenue history, and equipment value. Vac trucks have strong resale markets in regions with active utility and environmental work.

New vs. Used Vac Truck Financing

New vac trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down for qualified borrowers. Used vac truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Tank condition, vacuum specs, and chassis mileage affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older tanks. Vacuum excavator truck financing covers similar equipment with slightly different configurations.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Contract revenue, pumping revenue, or excavation rates. Steady revenue supports approval.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Tank capacity, vacuum specs, chassis age, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit affect rate and approval.

Down Payment and Terms

Down payments typically range from 10% to 30%. New vac trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Advance rates cap at 80–90% for new and 70–80% for used. Rates typically range from 7% to 15% APR for qualified borrowers.

Related Equipment

Bucket truck financing covers aerial work—some utility contractors use both bucket and vac trucks. Dump truck financing covers hauling; vac trucks often work with dump trucks for material disposal. Service truck financing covers support vehicles. Garbage truck financing covers refuse—different application but similar vocational structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vac truck?

A vac truck is a truck with a vacuum tank for excavation, industrial cleaning, or septic hauling. Includes hydro excavation, septic vac, and industrial vacuum trucks.

How much does a vac truck cost to finance?

Vac trucks range from $80,000 to $250,000+ depending on tank capacity and configuration. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used vac truck?

Yes. Used vac truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Tank condition and vacuum specs affect valuation.

What industries use vac trucks?

Environmental services, utilities, septic haulers, oilfield support, and industrial cleaning. Vac trucks are used for non-destructive excavation and material removal.
