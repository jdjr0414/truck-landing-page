Financing for Cement Trucks & Ready-Mix Mixers

Cement truck financing covers ready-mix trucks and mixer trucks used by concrete suppliers and construction contractors. Cement trucks combine a heavy-duty chassis with a rotating drum—lenders finance the complete unit. Both new and used cement trucks are financed by commercial lenders familiar with construction equipment.

Drum Capacity and Valuation

Cement truck value depends on drum capacity (cubic yards), chassis specs, and condition. Larger drums (10–12 yards) command higher values. Lenders familiar with construction equipment understand mixer specs. Document drum capacity, manufacturer, and chassis. Well-maintained drums retain value—inspect drum condition and rotation mechanism before purchase.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment. Higher-risk scenarios—startups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding credit—may require 20–30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business typically receive the best terms—often 10–15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15–25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20–30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.
  • Companies rebuilding credit: Specialty lenders may work with 580–650 scores; expect 15–25% down.

New vs. Used Cement Truck Financing

New cement trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used cement truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Drum condition and chassis mileage affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older mixers.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Concrete delivery revenue, contractor contracts, or project work.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Drum capacity, chassis, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit affect rate and approval.

Common Questions

What credit score is needed to finance a cement truck?

Most lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for the best terms. Concrete suppliers with delivery contracts may qualify with lower scores.

How much down payment is required for cement truck financing?

Typically 10–30%. New cement trucks often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with little or no down payment.

Can startups finance cement trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer concrete businesses. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts with contractors, and strong personal credit.

How long do cement truck loans usually last?

New cement trucks: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and drum condition. Drum capacity affects terms.

How quickly can cement truck financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days. Have business documentation and equipment details ready.

Can I finance a used cement truck?

Yes. Used cement truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Drum condition and chassis mileage affect valuation.

What documents are needed for cement truck financing?

Business tax returns (2 years), bank statements (3–6 months), driver's license, and equipment details (chassis, drum capacity, price). Proof of delivery contracts helps.

How much does a cement truck cost to finance?

Ready-mix cement trucks range from $150,000 to $350,000+ depending on drum capacity. Down payments typically run 10–30%. See how much does a cement truck cost.

Concrete suppliers, ready-mix companies, and construction contractors. Cement trucks deliver mixed concrete to job sites. Lenders evaluate delivery revenue and contractor contracts.
