Reefer units maintain temperature for perishable goods. Both straight truck reefers and tractor-trailer reefers are financed. Lenders evaluate chassis and reefer unit condition.

Reefer Unit and Valuation

Refrigerated truck value depends on reefer unit specs (capacity, temperature range), chassis, and condition. Reefer units can be mechanical or cryogenic. Document reefer manufacturer, model, and temperature specs. Reefer condition affects valuation—units that fail to hold temperature reduce resale value.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment. Higher-risk scenarios—startups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding credit—may require 20–30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business typically receive the best terms—often 10–15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15–25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20–30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.
  • Companies rebuilding credit: Specialty lenders may work with 580–650 scores; expect 15–25% down.

New vs. Used Refrigerated Truck Financing

New refrigerated trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used refrigerated truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Reefer unit age and condition affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older units.

Common Questions

What credit score is needed to finance a refrigerated truck?

Most lenders prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ typically qualifies for the best terms. Food distributors and cold chain businesses with delivery contracts may qualify with lower scores.

How much down payment is required for refrigerated truck financing?

Typically 10–30%. New reefers often allow 10–15%; used may require 20–30%. Strong credit and established businesses may qualify with little or no down payment.

Can startups finance refrigerated trucks?

Yes. Some lenders work with newer food delivery or logistics businesses. Expect 20–30% down, proof of contracts or routes, and strong personal credit.

How long do refrigerated truck loans usually last?

New reefers: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and reefer condition. Reefer units retain value when well-maintained.

How quickly can refrigerated truck financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days. Have business documentation and equipment details ready.

Can I finance a used refrigerated truck?

Yes. Used refrigerated truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months. Reefer condition and temperature specs affect valuation.

What documents are needed for refrigerated truck financing?

Business tax returns (2 years), bank statements (3–6 months), driver's license, and equipment details (chassis, reefer specs, size, price).

How much does a refrigerated truck cost to finance?

Refrigerated trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+ depending on size and reefer unit. Down payments typically run 10–30%. Reefers command premium value when well-maintained.

What is a reefer truck?

A reefer truck has a refrigerated cargo box or trailer. Reefer units maintain temperature for perishable or temperature-sensitive cargo. Food distributors and cold chain logistics commonly use reefers.
