Financing for Refrigerated Trucks & Cold Chain Vehicles

Refrigerated truck financing covers trucks with refrigerated cargo boxes—reefer trucks for food delivery, cold chain logistics, and temperature-sensitive cargo. Reefer units maintain temperature for perishable goods. Both straight truck reefers and tractor-trailer reefers are financed. Lenders evaluate chassis and reefer unit condition.

Who Needs Refrigerated Truck Financing?

Food distributors, cold chain logistics, grocery delivery, pharmaceutical haulers, and route-based food businesses. Revenue comes from delivery fees, contracts, or route revenue. Reefer trucks are essential for perishable cargo—they command premium value when well-maintained. Lenders evaluate business revenue, time in business, and equipment value.

Reefer Unit and Valuation

Refrigerated truck value depends on reefer unit specs (capacity, temperature range), chassis, and condition. Reefer units can be mechanical or cryogenic. Document reefer manufacturer, model, and temperature specs. Reefer condition affects valuation—units that fail to hold temperature reduce resale value.

New vs. Used Refrigerated Truck Financing

New refrigerated trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used refrigerated truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Reefer unit age and condition affect valuation. Lenders may require inspection for older units.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Delivery revenue, cold chain contracts, or route revenue.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Cargo size, reefer specs, chassis, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

Related Equipment

Box truck financing covers dry box trucks. Semi truck financing covers tractors for reefer trailers. Tanker truck financing covers liquid hauling—some food-grade tankers. Flatbed truck financing covers open beds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a refrigerated truck cost to finance?

Refrigerated trucks range from $60,000 to $150,000+ depending on size and reefer unit. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used refrigerated truck?

Yes. Used refrigerated truck financing is available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

Who uses refrigerated trucks?

Food distributors, cold chain logistics, grocery delivery, and pharmaceutical haulers. Reefer trucks maintain temperature-controlled cargo.

What is a reefer truck?

A reefer truck has a refrigerated cargo box or trailer. Reefer units maintain temperature for perishable or temperature-sensitive cargo.
