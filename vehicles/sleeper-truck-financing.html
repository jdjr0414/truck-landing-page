Sleeper Truck Financing

Financing for Sleeper Trucks & Long-Haul Tractors

Sleeper truck financing covers tractors with sleeper berths for long-haul and over-the-road freight. Sleeper trucks enable multi-day runs and cross-country hauling. Owner-operators and OTR fleets use sleeper tractors. Sleepers typically have higher acquisition costs than day cabs but command strong resale in long-haul markets.

Who Needs Sleeper Truck Financing?

Long-haul carriers, owner-operators, and over-the-road fleets. Sleeper trucks suit freight that requires multi-day runs—cross-country, regional long-haul, or dedicated lanes. Revenue comes from freight rates, load board, or contract hauling. Lenders evaluate business revenue, load history, and equipment value. Owner-operators may need proof of contracts or carrier agreements.

Sleeper vs. Day Cab

Day cab semi financing covers tractors without sleepers. Sleeper trucks have berths for driver rest—required for long-haul. Sleeper cabs vary in size (condo, mid-roof, flat-top). Financing structures are similar; sleepers often have higher acquisition cost and stronger resale in OTR markets.

New vs. Used Sleeper Truck Financing

New sleeper trucks qualify for 60–84 month terms and 10–15% down. Used sleeper truck financing typically runs 36–60 months with 20–30% down. Mileage is a key factor—OTR tractors accumulate miles quickly. Lenders consider age, mileage, and remaining useful life.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Revenue: Freight revenue, load history, or carrier contracts.
  • Time in business: 12–24 months minimum; 2+ years for stronger terms.
  • Equipment: Chassis, sleeper size, mileage, and condition.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleeper truck?

A sleeper truck is a tractor with a sleeper berth for driver rest. Used for long-haul and over-the-road freight.

How much does a sleeper truck cost to finance?

Sleeper tractors range from $120,000 to $200,000+ new. Down payments typically run 10–30%.

Can I finance a used sleeper truck?

Yes. Used sleeper truck financing is widely available. Terms are typically 36–60 months.

Who uses sleeper trucks?

Long-haul carriers, owner-operators, and OTR fleets. Sleeper trucks enable multi-day runs and cross-country freight.
