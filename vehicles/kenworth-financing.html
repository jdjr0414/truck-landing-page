PACCAR Financial (Captive Financing)

PACCAR Financial is the captive finance arm for Kenworth and Peterbilt trucks. It offers retail financing and leasing through authorized Kenworth dealers. Benefits include promotional rates on new equipment, streamlined approval for dealer purchases, and programs tailored to owner-operators and fleets. Captive programs typically favor new truck purchases; used truck financing may have stricter age and mileage limits. Compare PACCAR Financial with independent lenders—captive programs can be strong for new dealer purchases, while independents may offer more flexibility on used equipment or credit.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Companies with strong credit and established revenue often qualify with little or no down payment through PACCAR Financial or independent lenders. Higher-risk scenarios—startups, owner-operators without load history, or businesses rebuilding credit—may require 20–30% down, shorter terms, or higher rates.

  • Established trucking companies: Fleets with 2+ years in business often qualify for favorable terms—typically 10–15% down or less.
  • Owner-operators: May qualify with carrier agreements or load history. Down payments of 15–25% are common.
  • Startups: Often need 20–30% down, a business plan, and proof of contracts.
  • Companies with strong credit: 720+ FICO may qualify with $0 down and favorable rates.

New vs. Used Kenworth Financing

New Kenworth trucks qualify for longer terms (60–84 months), lower rates, and higher advance rates through PACCAR Financial. Promotional rates are often available on dealer purchases. Kenworth's strong resale value can support favorable used truck financing. Used Kenworth financing is more restrictive than new: shorter terms (36–60 months), higher rates, and lower advances. Lenders consider mileage, age, and remaining useful life.

What Lenders Evaluate

  • Time in business: Most prefer 12–24 months minimum; stronger programs require 2+ years.
  • Revenue: Annual revenue and cash flow support the payment. Owner-operators may provide load history or contracts.
  • Credit: Personal and business credit affect rate and approval. Scores of 650+ typically qualify for competitive terms.
  • Equipment: Age, mileage, condition, and resale value. Kenworth's premium resale can support better terms on used equipment.

Financing Terms

Terms run 60–84 months for new and 36–60 months for used. Rates typically range from 7% to 15% APR for qualified commercial borrowers. Down payments are risk-based—strong credit and established businesses may qualify with no down payment.

Mileage and Age Limits for Used Kenworths

Lenders impose mileage and age limits on used Kenworth financing. Kenworth's strong resale value can support favorable limits—some programs allow 7 years or 700,000 miles. The T680 and W990 hold value well in the used market. Older or higher-mileage units may face shorter terms or higher rates. Check the truck's odometer and model year before applying.

Operating Cost Considerations

Kenworth trucks have operating costs similar to other premium Class 8 tractors. The T680's aerodynamic design supports fuel efficiency. Budget for $2,000–$6,000 monthly fuel, $800–$2,500 insurance, and $500–$2,000 maintenance. Kenworth's dealer network provides strong parts and service support nationwide.

Common Questions

How much does a Kenworth truck typically cost?

New Kenworth T680: $140,000–$200,000. T880 and W990 vary. Used: $55,000–$140,000 depending on model and mileage.

What credit score is needed for Kenworth financing?

PACCAR Financial and independent lenders typically prefer 600+ for competitive rates. 720+ qualifies for the best terms.

Does Kenworth offer manufacturer financing?

Yes. PACCAR Financial provides captive financing for Kenworth trucks purchased through authorized dealers.

How long are Kenworth truck financing terms?

New: 60–84 months. Used: 36–60 months depending on age and mileage.

Can I finance a used Kenworth?

Yes. Both PACCAR Financial and independent lenders finance used Kenworth trucks within age and mileage limits.

Can I finance a Kenworth as an owner-operator?

Yes. Owner-operators can finance Kenworth trucks. Lenders may require proof of contracts, load history, or carrier agreements.

How quickly can Kenworth financing be approved?

Pre-approval: 24–72 hours. Full approval and funding: typically 1–5 business days.
