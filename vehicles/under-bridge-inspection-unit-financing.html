Definition

An under-bridge inspection unit (UBIU) is a specialized vehicle equipped with an articulating platform that extends beneath bridges to provide access for inspectors. UBIUs are used by state DOTs, engineering firms, bridge inspection contractors, and infrastructure maintenance companies. They eliminate the need for lane closures or scaffolding. UBIUs require a Class B CDL and often operator certification for the inspection platform.

Typical Financing Scenarios

Financing terms vary by borrower profile. Established bridge inspection contractors with DOT contracts often qualify for favorable terms. Higher-risk scenarios—startups or businesses without contracts—may require 25–35% down.

  • Established contractors: 3+ years with DOT contracts often qualify for 20–25% down, 96-month terms.
  • Growing operators: May qualify with 25–30% down and proof of contracts or revenue.
  • Startups: Often need 30–35% down, business plan, and proof of contracts or letters of intent.
  • Government/Municipal: May qualify for specialized government financing or lease programs.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underestimating reach requirements: Different bridges require different platform reaches; ensure the unit matches your contract scope.
  • Not securing contracts before purchase: Lenders prefer proof of revenue; having DOT or engineering contracts strengthens applications.
  • Ignoring platform certification requirements: Operators may need specialized training or certification for the inspection platform.
  • Overlooking maintenance and inspection costs: UBIUs have complex hydraulic and structural systems; factor ongoing maintenance and certification into operating costs.

Licensing and Regulatory Requirements

Licensing requirements for operating an under-bridge inspection unit vary by state and business activity. The following is general guidance—businesses should verify requirements with their state motor vehicle agency, the FMCSA, and equipment manufacturers.

Driver License Requirements

Under-bridge inspection units typically exceed 26,000 lbs GVWR and require a Class B CDL. Platform operators may need additional certification or training per manufacturer requirements. Requirements vary by state.

Common Questions

Do you need a CDL to operate an under-bridge inspection unit?

Yes. Under-bridge inspection units typically exceed 26,000 lbs GVWR and require a Class B CDL. Platform operator certification may also be required.

How much does an under-bridge inspection unit cost to finance?

Under-bridge inspection units range from $500,000 to $1,500,000+ new. Down payments typically run 20–35%.

Can I finance a used under-bridge inspection unit?

Yes. Used under-bridge inspection unit financing is available. Terms are typically 60–84 months for newer units.

Who uses under-bridge inspection units?

DOT agencies, engineering firms, bridge inspection contractors, and infrastructure maintenance companies.

What credit score is needed to finance an under-bridge inspection unit?

How much down payment is required for under-bridge inspection unit financing?

Typically 20–35%. New units often require 20–25%; used may require 25–35%.

Can startups finance under-bridge inspection units?

Yes, but it is challenging. Expect 30–35% down, proof of contracts or letters of intent, and a strong business plan.

How long do under-bridge inspection unit loans usually last?

New: 84–120 months. Used: 60–84 months depending on age and condition.

What documents are needed for under-bridge inspection unit financing?

Business tax returns (2–3 years), bank statements, DOT or engineering contracts, and equipment details.

What is the difference between under-bridge inspection unit and bucket truck?

UBIUs have platforms that extend beneath bridges for inspection. Bucket trucks have aerial buckets for overhead work. Both require CDL and specialized operation.
