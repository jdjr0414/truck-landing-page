Quick Answer

Fleet truck financing covers multiple trucks or replacement cycles. Lenders may offer volume pricing, streamlined documentation, and master agreements. Fleet programs typically start at 2+ units and can provide better rates than single-unit purchases.

Common Questions

What credit score do fleets need for truck financing?

Many fleet programs prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Established fleets with strong revenue may qualify for better rates.

How much down payment do fleet programs require?

Typically 10–30% per unit. Fleet programs may offer flexibility. Strong credit and established revenue can reduce requirements.

Can startup fleets get financing?

Less common. Fleet programs typically require 2+ units and established operations. New fleets may start with single-unit financing.

How long are fleet loan terms?

New trucks: 60–84 months. Used: 24–60 months. Master agreements may allow consistent terms across replacement cycles.

How fast can fleet financing be approved?

Streamlined documentation can speed approval. Simple fleet additions: 1–5 business days. New relationships: 1–2 weeks.

Can fleets finance used trucks?

Yes. Fleet replacement programs often include used equipment. Terms are shorter than for new. Volume pricing may apply.

What documentation do fleets need?

Business tax returns, fleet financials, bank statements, and equipment details. Master agreements may reduce documentation for repeat purchases.

How many trucks qualify as a fleet?

Typically 2+ units. Some programs start at 3–5 units. Fleet programs vary by lender.
