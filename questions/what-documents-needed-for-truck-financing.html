What Documents Are Needed for Truck Financing?

Truck financing documents typically include: driver's license, 2 years of personal and business tax returns, 3–6 months of bank statements, business formation documents, proof of insurance (or binder), and an equipment quote or invoice. Startups may need a business plan. Having documents ready speeds approval. This guide provides a complete checklist.

Common Questions

What credit score do I need for truck financing?

Many lenders prefer 650+ for competitive terms. Some work with 580–650 with higher down payments. Documents support your profile.

How much down payment with my documents?

Strong revenue and credit in your docs may qualify for $0 down. Weak profile may require 25–35%. Down payments are risk-based.

Do startups need different documents?

Startups may need business plan, carrier agreements, and proof of contracts. Established businesses may need less.

How long does approval take with complete documents?

Complete docs speed approval. Pre-approval: same-day to 48 hours. Full approval: 1–5 business days.

Do I need different documents for used vs new trucks?

Same core docs. Used may require inspection or appraisal. Equipment details (year, mileage, condition) matter for used.

What documents do I need for a truck loan?

Driver's license, 2 years tax returns, 3–6 months bank statements, business formation docs, proof of insurance, and equipment quote.

What documents do I need for equipment financing?

Same as truck financing: tax returns, bank statements, P&L, business docs, and equipment details. Some programs require less.

Do I need a business plan for truck financing?

Startups may need a business plan. Established businesses often need less.

Can I get truck financing with bad credit?

Some lenders work with lower credit. Expect more documentation and higher down payment.
