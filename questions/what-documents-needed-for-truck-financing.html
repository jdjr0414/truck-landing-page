What Documents Are Needed for Truck Financing?

Truck financing documents typically include: driver's license, 2 years of personal and business tax returns, 3–6 months of bank statements, business formation documents, proof of insurance (or binder), and an equipment quote or invoice. Startups may need a business plan. Having documents ready speeds approval. This guide provides a complete checklist.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for a truck loan?

Driver's license, 2 years tax returns, 3–6 months bank statements, business formation docs, proof of insurance, and equipment quote.

Do I need a business plan for truck financing?

Startups may need a business plan. Established businesses often need less.

How long does truck financing approval take?

Same-day to 24–48 hours for pre-approval. Full approval with funding: 1–5 business days.

Can I get truck financing with bad credit?

Some lenders work with lower credit. Expect more documentation and higher down payment.
