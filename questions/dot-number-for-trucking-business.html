DOT Number for Trucking Business

A USDOT number is a unique identifier assigned by the FMCSA to commercial motor carriers. Trucking businesses operating in interstate commerce or meeting state intrastate thresholds typically need one. This guide covers when you need a DOT number and how to obtain it.

Register through the FMCSA Unified Registration System–there is no fee. Processing typically takes 1–2 business days.

When Do You Need a DOT Number?

You need a USDOT number if:

  • You operate commercial motor vehicles in interstate commerce.
  • Your vehicles have a GVWR of 10,001+ lbs and are used in interstate commerce.
  • Your state requires DOT registration for intrastate operations (check your state's weight thresholds).
  • You transport hazardous materials in quantities requiring placarding.
  • You transport 9+ passengers (including driver) for compensation.

How to Obtain a DOT Number

Register through the FMCSA Unified Registration System (URS) at fmcsa.dot.gov. You will need business information (legal name, address, EIN or SSN), vehicle information, and operation type. There is no fee for obtaining a USDOT number. Processing typically takes 1–2 business days.

Disclaimer: DOT requirements vary by state. Verify with the FMCSA and your state agency.

Common Questions

Does my trucking business need a DOT number?

If you operate in interstate commerce or your state requires it for intrastate operations, yes. Many states require DOT registration for vehicles over 26,000 lbs.

How do I get a DOT number?

Register online through the FMCSA Unified Registration System. No fee for USDOT number.

Is there a fee for a DOT number?

No. There is no fee for obtaining a USDOT number.

How long does it take to get a DOT number?

Online registration typically takes 1–2 business days for processing.

Do owner-operators need a DOT number?

Yes, if operating in interstate commerce or if required by state for intrastate operations.
