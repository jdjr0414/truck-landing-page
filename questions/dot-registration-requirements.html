DOT Registration Requirements

Businesses that operate commercial motor vehicles in interstate commerce must register with the U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) and obtain a USDOT number through the FMCSA. Intrastate requirements vary by state. This guide covers when DOT registration is required and how to comply.

Key Takeaways

  • DOT registration required for interstate commerce; intrastate often required for heavy vehicles
  • Register through FMCSA Unified Registration System; no fee for USDOT number
  • For-hire hauling requires MC authority in addition to USDOT
  • Register or verify at FMCSA

Register through the FMCSA Unified Registration System. Intrastate operations depend on state regulations—many states require DOT registration for heavy vehicles even for local-only operations. There is no fee for obtaining a USDOT number. Vehicle weight, cargo type, and passenger transport can affect requirements.

When Is DOT Registration Required?

Federal regulations (administered by the FMCSA under DOT) require DOT registration for:

  • Commercial motor vehicles used in interstate commerce.
  • Vehicles with a GVWR of 10,001+ lbs or GVWR of 10,001+ lbs when used in interstate commerce.
  • Vehicles designed to transport 9+ passengers (including driver) for compensation.
  • Vehicles transporting hazardous materials in quantities requiring placarding.

Intrastate-only operations: Requirements vary by state. Many states require DOT registration for vehicles over a certain weight (e.g., 26,000 lbs GVWR) regardless of interstate use.

How to Register

Register through the FMCSA Unified Registration System (URS) at fmcsa.dot.gov. You will need:

  • Business information (legal name, address, EIN or SSN).
  • Vehicle information (type, weight).
  • Operation type (interstate, intrastate).

There is no fee for obtaining a USDOT number. Processing typically takes 1–2 business days for online applications.

Disclaimer: DOT registration requirements vary by state and operation type. Verify with the FMCSA and your state agency.

Common Questions

When is DOT registration required?

DOT registration is required for interstate commerce. Intrastate operations depend on state regulations.

What is a USDOT number?

A unique identifier assigned by the FMCSA to track commercial motor vehicles and carriers.

Do local trucking companies need DOT registration?

Often yes if operating heavy vehicles. Intrastate rules vary by state.
